# The Care Web v88.4.0 — blocks and automation register

The searchable source register has been regenerated for this release. See [PAYMENT-SETUP-v88.4.0.md](PAYMENT-SETUP-v88.4.0.md) for current payment operation and [PAYMENT-ALERT-COVERAGE-v88.4.0.md](PAYMENT-ALERT-COVERAGE-v88.4.0.md) for the 41 alert cases.

| Payment condition | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| New completed self-managed/private shift | Invoice and email queue immediately; no extra office billing or holiday sign-off. |
| Participant has not explicitly reviewed the shift | The invoice exists; online collection opens after the participant or authorised bookings helper approves. A payment-link recipient cannot approve care. |
| Participant queries a shift | Collection and reminders pause for that invoice until explicit review; a worker reply does not approve it. |
| Online provider is unconfigured | Shift and invoice persist; approved invoices can show configured ordinary bank details. Optional provider setup is done once. |
| Checkout temporarily fails | The durable request retries; the approved shift remains saved. |
| Previous checkout is still payable | Its cancellation retries before a second checkout can collect money. A payment already processing waits for its actual outcome. |
| Provider request outcome remains uncertain past its safe retry window | Office sees a named payment-confirmation task. The site does not blindly create a second charge. |
| Customer pays through a known card/PayTo checkout | Signed Stripe confirmation automatically links the payment to its invoice and applies the valid amount once. |
| Historical or exceptional money needs reconciliation | Existing receipt stays visible; office checks the evidence and matches any eligible unallocated amount. It is never matched by amount alone. |
| Payment belongs to a different participant or provider environment | Allocation is refused; a sandbox receipt cannot settle a production invoice. |
| Refund/dispute or unsupported financial event | Visible reconciliation task; no automatic refund or unverified ledger reversal. |
| Payroll export | Does not transfer wages. Existing component review and separate approval remain; external results are labelled as office records. |

These are payment-state and access checks, not recurring finance/holiday setup approvals. Ordinary approval, invoicing, payment confirmation, receipt delivery and reminder recovery are automatic after provider activation. Worker unfinished-visit and ageing-dispute escalation, a connected payroll feed and onward settlement confirmation retain the limitations documented in the alert coverage.

## Earlier automation changes retained

This release removes the finance-approval and reviewed-holiday-calendar barriers introduced in v88.3.0–88.3.1. A normal approved shift now proceeds to invoice preparation and queued email without an office billing run. The automatic NSW calendar also supplies business-day deadlines and standby holiday classification.

This document explains the business decisions. **The complete searchable source register is `block-register/index.html`.** It lists each detected error, eligibility item, confirmation, false decision, required catalogue field, database constraint and unavailable control with its file, line, trigger and source. `register.csv` and `register.json` contain the same entries. `branches.csv` and `branches.json` additionally enumerate all conditional expressions, short-circuit checks, switch cases, returns, throws, breaks and continues—including silent exits that do not contain a message. Duplicate policy enforcement at different endpoints is intentionally visible.

The register covers the supplied application's first-party code and deployment/maintenance scripts. It does not inspect production accounts, secrets, current hosting settings or private external systems. Returned labels, hidden panels and successful early returns are not necessarily blocks; the register identifies their type instead of claiming every source branch is a launch blocker. Exact counts and hashes are recorded in `block-register/register.json`. Dependencies and test fixtures are excluded from the application census.

## Removed or automated in this release

| Previous stop or delay | New behavior |
|---|---|
| A09 financial review absent or expired | No blanket invoice hold. The audit record remains available as evidence; its status does not disable ordinary billing. |
| No reviewed holiday calendar covering the shift | Automatic NSW public-holiday lookup; no coverage confirmation form. |
| A public holiday itself | The appropriate holiday rate and support item are calculated automatically. |
| A visit crosses midnight, a price band or daylight saving | The charge is itemised by local date and rate. Duration uses actual elapsed time. |
| Ordinary sleepover, recorded extra active hours, travel, cancellation or shared-support line automatically referred to finance just because of its type | The existing supported charging inputs calculate their lines; these types alone no longer generate an additional finance-review hold. Actual charge corrections remain possible. |
| Private-billing “reviewed and ready” switch | No extra approval gate. Private invoices use the configured tax treatment; the office can change that business setting. Installing this release does not establish whether a particular service is taxable. |
| Participant approves, then waits for a nightly invoice run | Approval queues processing immediately; a recovery sweep runs every 15 seconds. |
| An invoice job fails or the process restarts | Durable per-booking queue with automatic retries; approved unissued visits are recovered. |
| Email waits for a digest or quiet hours | Transactional invoice email is queued for immediate delivery with its PDF. |
| Five invoice-email failures force a manual retry | Automatic invoice retries continue with backoff capped at one hour. Persistent failure remains visible to the office. Routine non-invoice messages retain their existing five-attempt policy. |
| Repeated participant approval returns an error | Approval is idempotent: the existing approval and invoice are returned without creating another invoice. |
| A valid item looked wrong because unrelated setup notes were red | Readiness notes stay separate from the support-item input. The claims summary shows automatic billing and separate delivery status. |
| Incident/complaint business-day calendars and standby holiday bands omit automatic holiday detection | NSW state holidays are supplied automatically. Additional incident-calendar dates can still be recorded if applicable. Existing filed incident evidence is preserved. |

**Installation starts the automation.** Existing approved, unissued, non-voided visits are eligible for recovery processing after restart. An explicit invoice hold, disputed timesheet or invalid billing information is still respected. Existing approved amounts and issued invoice snapshots are not silently repriced by the new calendar. A correction uses the existing charge/withdrawal/receipt controls.

## Automatic calendar scope and calculation limits

The supplied holiday integration is for **NSW**, the site's existing default service jurisdiction. It contains the published 2026–27 state calendar and local holiday table, refreshes both daily, and keeps the previous valid response if the source fails or its format changes. Future state dates have a statutory-rule fallback. Local dates use the recorded place name and published hours; a full street address, ambiguous part-locality or council map boundary is not a geocoded location. Keep the participant's service suburb accurate. A pre-existing non-NSW configured calendar can still be used; nationwide official feeds and council-boundary geocoding are not implemented. This is a disclosed coverage limit, not a hidden “holiday reviewed” switch. [NSW state holidays](https://www.nsw.gov.au/about-nsw/public-holidays), [NSW local holidays](https://www.nsw.gov.au/about-nsw/public-holidays/local-public-holidays).

Ordinary timed supports are split into appropriate date/time lines. Fixed-price household/employment categories and flat sleepovers retain their specific existing models. Additional sleepover active support uses the recorded sleepover date, a weekday minimum of the Saturday limit, and Sunday/public-holiday categories where applicable. Detailed active-period timestamps are not captured by the existing form; complex timing still needs a correct recorded charge. The release does not decide clinical eligibility, cancellation entitlement or individual service-agreement terms by inference. The published national price schedule is not automatically every participant's agreed price. [NDIS pricing arrangements and current schedule](https://www.ndis.gov.au/providers/pricing-and-payments/pricing/pricing-arrangements).

Invoice/outbox event IDs prevent duplicates from repeated approval and catch-up runs. Resend requests now include a stable idempotency key. Resend documents a **24-hour** deduplication window; SMTP generally cannot promise exactly-once delivery after an ambiguous network failure. The queued/sent status is a transport state, not proof that a person read the message. [Resend idempotency documentation](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/emails/idempotency-keys).

## What is still a person’s decision

The remaining major manual decisions are: participant consent and disputed timesheets; worker identity/clearance/employment suitability; support-plan and excluded-support review; uncertain actual delivery/late cover; deliberately held or corrected charges; payroll and refund approval; external bank/NDIA transactions; incident submission evidence; and optional AI processor consent/assessment. These are explicitly named in the register. There is no new holiday-review approval step.

Daily administration already automated includes queues, reminders, verification assignment, approved combined follow-ups, renewal tasks, calendar cancellation updates, request badges, cover cascades, timesheet reminders/deemed approval, invoice preparation, invoice-email retries and eligible referral progression. Payroll drafts require the existing cutover setting to avoid paying historical work twice. This release does not claim that external portals, clinical judgments or bank payments have been automated when their authorised integrations do not exist.

## v88.3.3: task clarity and website monitoring

Task colour is a responsibility/urgency label, not a new gate. Office actions, waiting-on-person tasks and website alerts are separated. The old manual certificate review reminder is removed. A public HTTPS certificate check runs automatically from APP_URL, retries connection failures hourly and clears alerts after recovery. Missing public URL configuration, invalid certificates, approaching expiry, repeated connection failure and stale monitoring are explicit website alerts; none gates booking or invoicing. Hosting still performs certificate renewal. Participant first-visit feedback is optional and has no office assignment step.

## v88.3.4: invoice flow

Invoice withdrawal is a deliberate billing hold, cleared by explicit Release. It now works through the shared confirmation dialog. Queued demand mail is cancelled and a withdrawal notice goes to the original payer. Issued snapshots and numbers are retained. Received or unmatched payments require reconciliation before withdrawal; per-shift paid controls cannot bypass that ledger. Card checkout retirement is automatic and retries, with completed/racing payments retained for reconciliation. An already-uploaded NDIA claim must still be withdrawn in its provider portal. New website task layout and automatic certificate checks from v88.3.3 are included.
