# Money and page navigation — v88.4.8

The Money sidebar now has five destinations instead of ten. Each opens a focused view, with short section links for its related tools.

| Main destination | Sections |
| --- | --- |
| Invoices & payments | Invoices; Received payments; Invoice history & withdrawal |
| Charges | Unissued charges; Additional charges |
| Needs attention | Payment exceptions; Corrections & refunds; Finance evidence |
| NDIA claims | Claim files and recorded claim payments |
| Worker pay | Pay batches; Prepare a batch |

Payment connections remains under Settings. It is setup, rather than a daily payment task. The tools remain searchable by their individual names. Old links remain usable.

Invoice review and payment actions, historical receipts, withdrawals, unissued-charge processing, charge corrections, refunds, claim submission records and payroll review retain their existing controls and permissions. This update does not approve invoices, initiate payments or alter rates.

Select a person in People → Verification to bring their file panel into view. Switching related tabs and record pages keeps the workspace in view. Navigation waits for the selected content and discards stale position updates after navigation or account changes.

Install this current-only ZIP over v88.4.7, merge the files into the existing repository, redeploy and refresh the browser. No new database schema or separate website is required.
