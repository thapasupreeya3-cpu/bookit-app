# Deployment acceptance — record actual evidence

Use separate named office accounts. Run in authorised staging with fictional records and provider test modes. Do not mark evidence complete because an automated local suite passed. Each row needs person, date/build, environment, result, evidence link, defect and retest.

## Release decision
Record the build, deployed hostname, accountable sign-off, unresolved defects, continuity coverage, actual service scope and rollback decision. Launch checks in the app store evidence and owners; they do not submit regulator forms, issue legal approval, independently inspect TLS, or guarantee off-site backup delivery. Optional research pilots are not required to pretend launch work is complete.
