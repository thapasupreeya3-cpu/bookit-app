# Research-led pilots — proposed, disabled until separately accepted

The prior audit bibliography is launch-research-sources.json. These are bounded evaluation protocols, not a claim that new models, passkeys or clinical decision tools are deployed. Existing manual routes remain usable.

| Pilot | Concrete design | Measures / stop conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Passkeys (A39) | Small opt-in WebAuthn staging cohort only after MFA recovery fixes pass; validate RP ID/origin, challenge lifecycle, user verification, cross-device use and lost-device recovery. Independent security review before coding into production. | Successful sign-in/recovery by supported browser and assistive tech; no downgraded recovery or locked-out cohort. Stop on account confusion or bypass. |
| Document suggestions (A40) | Use the existing consented extraction/review flow with an assessed processor and synthetic/de-identified fixture set, including poor scans. Retain manual entry. Reviewer compares every field with source; never auto-approve from confidence. | Field-level exactness, unsupported values, review time, correction rate, refusal and withdrawal. Stop on private-data routing error, invented evidence or unsafe date use. |
| Reminder usefulness (A41) | Compare existing timing/wording with one bounded alternative after approval. Respect language/contact needs and opt-out; keep urgent safeguards constant. Do not withhold critical reminders for experimentation. | Actual delivery, task resolution, contacts per resolution, complaints, accessibility/effort, office escalation and subgroup barriers. Stop on stale/duplicate/pressuring reminders. |
| Worker-authored note assistance (A42) | Research only: worker supplies facts, assistance may organise or proofread; no generated observations, clinical inference or autonomous completion. Require explicit review and provenance before any trial integration. | Unsupported clinical facts must be zero; measure omissions, editing burden, consent/privacy, correction/addendum rate. Stop on invented care or copied wrong-participant facts. |
| Inclusive usability (A43) | Compensated, consented participant/worker/helper sessions in accessible formats. Offer breaks, plain-language instructions and support-person choice. Five key tasks: identify missing evidence, replace one file, respond to booking, find/return from calendar visit, recover failed note save. | Completion without prompting, time, errors, confidence and reported effort; record which feedback changes the product. Retest all critical barriers. |

Decision record per pilot: hypothesis, existing evidence and limitations, participants/consent, safety/privacy review, measure definitions, baseline, minimum acceptable outcome, duration/cap, owner, stop rule, results, decision and outstanding uncertainty. No clinical effectiveness or accessibility claim follows from a small local code test.
