## Deliberate boundaries

Geography is an explicit service-area match, not an external map, routing or postcode database. Travel buffer is a declared scheduling margin, not a prediction of road time. Old visits are not automatically cancelled, corrected or erased. Current-plan acknowledgements are version-specific; existing legacy acknowledgements are retained.

The initial guide-only concerns about statutory incident clocks, award payroll and general record-retention policy are **not certified or silently rewritten by this source-review release**. Today displays the incident deadlines already held by The Care Web; this does not validate those deadlines against legislation. Actual registrations, employee arrangements, pricing rules and retention schedules require an authorised operational/legal review. No off-site backups, production monitoring service or real restore exercise was configured; those were not actions performed against your host.
