IMPORTANT MATCHING CHANGE
Workers must confirm Settings > Availability and service areas. An empty area
list now matches ONLY the worker's own profile suburb, not all of Australia.
Set exact suburb/state names or explicit postcodes. Missing locations do not
silently match everyone. No road-distance or travel-time lookup is implied.
Specific time windows and leave are optional; legacy usual-weekday availability
is labelled as such. Travel buffers are worker-declared minutes.
Existing accepted visits are NOT automatically cancelled by this migration.
Use node scripts/review-existing-data.js /actual/path/bookit.db ON THE
AUTHORISED HOST for a read-only triage list. Do not copy real data to test tools.

MIGRATION AND SAFETY
Boot adds columns/indexes plus shift_note_drafts. It does not drop tables,
rewrite delivered notes or mark any unpaid record as paid. Historical invalid
visits and questionable referral payments need office review, not silent repair.
Unpaid referrals below the current qualifying threshold are held from payroll.
Past paid evidence remains. Current-plan permissions take effect immediately.
