WHAT CHANGED IN v86.3.0 — THE PRIVACY CONSENT GETS THE AGREEMENT'S TREATMENT
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The Privacy and information-sharing consent was still DMHC's printed form:
  twelve tick-boxes, dotted lines for "share with" and "never share with",
  three signature blocks and a worker's declaration. It is now rendered on
  screen like the Service Agreement — the same for everyone, nothing to
  fill in — and published without a login at /privacy-consent, linked from
  the Privacy & terms page and from clause 9 of the agreement.

  DMHC's substance is kept in the same order. The tick-boxes and the dotted
  lines become the settings the account already has, and clause 8 of the
  consent says where each one is: Photo and media consent (photos, video,
  audio); Emails & notifications (newsletters and marketing, off unless
  turned on); People with access (sharing with named people or providers);
  "anyone we must never share with" (tell us, recorded on the file); the
  worker-access switch under My documents; and surveys and research, always
  optional. The signature page is the click record. Edition "privacy-consent
  v1, issued 23/08/2026".

  Anyone who agreed to the PDF is asked for a fresh click. The reset script
  clears the walkthrough account's earlier clicks, so after reset + prefill
  it shows one acceptance of the agreement and one of the consent, both
  current. The PDF stays on the shelf as the text of earlier acceptances.
  To change the wording, edit privacyConsentBody() and bump
  PRIVACY_CONSENT_EDITION.

  No migration. SCHEMA_VERSION stays 86000.


WHAT CHANGED IN v86.2.3 — THE PRIVACY CONSENT IS ITS OWN DOCUMENT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  The Privacy and information-sharing consent is a separate document with
  its own click, and the Service Agreement no longer repeats it: clause 9
  says we handle information under the Privacy Policy and that consent is
  given separately in that document. One consent, recorded once, in DMHC's
  own words. (The v86.2.1 note below said clause 9 "says the same thing";
  it no longer does.)

  The "We have updated the Service Agreement" panel on the walkthrough
  account is there only because that account agreed to the printed PDF and
  then to the personalised edition before the platform edition existed.
  The reset script clears it. A new participant never sees it; it only ever
  appears again if the agreement's text is changed after people have
  agreed to it, which is the one time asking again is right.

  No migration. SCHEMA_VERSION stays 86000.


WHAT CHANGED IN v86.2.2 — THE DOCUMENTS PAGE SPEAKS TO THE PARTICIPANT
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Every explanation on the participant's documents page and the bookings-page
  documents card was written for the person building the page, not the
  person using it: "2 of 3 of yours on file … most of the rest are ours to
  write", "each one is a click", "never counted against you", "ours to do,
  nothing for you to send", "nothing to fill in", and the internal edition
  strings ("service-agreement v2, on-screen edition, issued …"). All of it
  is gone or rewritten in the participant's words:

    Agreements card: the document, "agreed <date>", Read. Earlier versions
      fold under "earlier versions" with the date and Read — no edition
      strings on the page. (They are still recorded; the office sees them.)
    A re-issued agreement: "<Document> — We have updated the Service
      Agreement. Please read the new version and agree to it again. Your
      supports continue as normal." then "Read the new version · I agree".
    The file: "Only you and our office can see your documents." then the
      rows, then "Print any time: Your supports and prices · Intake form ·
      Emergency plan".
    Not on file yet: headings only — "For you to read and agree to", "We
      prepare these with you", "Written by your doctor or clinician", "Our
      records", "Optional", "Not needed for you (n)". Rows say Read ·
      I agree, or Blank form.
    "What a worker can read" and "Who manages this account": shortened to
      what the participant needs to decide.

  Two bugs fixed on the way: the "applies to you — your plan says so" badge
  was showing on every row, not only the plan-dependent ones; and a
  supports-and-prices snapshot was labelled with its internal key
  ("p-schedule") because the key is no longer in the participant catalogue.

  No migration. SCHEMA_VERSION stays 86000.


WHAT CHANGED IN v86.2.1 — THE PRIVACY CONSENT IS BACK, AND EVERYTHING IS v1
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE PRIVACY AND INFORMATION-SHARING CONSENT IS A REQUIRED CLICK AGAIN
    v86.2 had folded it into clause 9 of the Service Agreement and made the
    form optional. It is DMHC's own consent wording and an explicit record
    of consent is worth having, so it is asked for beside the agreement as
    before: read it, then I agree. Clause 9 now says the consent is
    recorded separately in our own words, so the two always match.

  THE SERVICE AGREEMENT'S EDITION READS v1
    The platform edition is the first edition anyone is asked to accept
    from here on, so it is labelled "v1, issued 22/08/2026" rather than v3.
    Any acceptance of the printed PDF or the short-lived personalised
    edition still reads as "needs a fresh click", because those people did
    not read this text.

  A RESET SCRIPT FOR THE TEST ACCOUNT — scripts/reset-test-agreements.js
    The walkthrough account has agreed to three editions in one day, which
    would show as "2 earlier editions" on Monday. The script clears ONE
    account's agreement clicks (agreement, privacy consent, old schedule
    clicks) and nothing else, refuses demo accounts and accounts with a
    completed booking, and does nothing without --yes. Run on the server
    with the site stopped, exactly like the conversion script; then run the
    prefill again and the account shows a single clean acceptance of each.

  No migration. SCHEMA_VERSION stays 86000.


WHAT CHANGED IN v86.2.0 — ONE AGREEMENT, NOTHING TO FILL IN
-----------------------------------------------------------
v86.1 rewrote the Service Agreement for the person, with their name, NDIS
number and funding arrangement filled in from the file. That still made
the agreement depend on fields, and it still read as a document about one
participant that somebody had to complete. The established platforms do
not do that: one agreement, the same for everyone, prices in a pricing
policy, the person's own supports in their plan and their bookings.

  THE SERVICE AGREEMENT IS NOW A PLATFORM AGREEMENT
    Twelve clauses and a glossary: how BookIt works; access (agree on
    screen, 18+ or a recorded parent/guardian, representatives); shared,
    your and our responsibilities (verification and clearances, workers
    are our employees, incidents, continuity, emergencies); consent to
    supports; bookings (availability, making, cancellation windows,
    location and travel, removal of a worker, offline arrangements);
    feedback, complaints and the Commission; fees (the Pricing page is the
    Pricing Policy; seven days' notice of a change; continued use is
    acceptance; timesheet approval; payment by funding arrangement;
    disputed fees; GST); ending; information management (privacy consent
    is clause 9); risk allocation; general; the policies table.
    Nothing on it belongs to one participant. Published without a login at
    /service-agreement and linked from the Privacy & terms page. Accepted
    by click on the documents page; the click records who, when and which
    edition and keeps a copy of the page. Edition: "service-agreement v3,
    platform edition, issued 22/08/2026". Anyone who agreed to the PDF (v1)
    or the personalised edition (v2) is asked for a fresh click; their
    earlier acceptances stay on file and stay readable.

  THE SCHEDULE OF SUPPORTS IS A PRINTOUT, NOT A SIGNATURE
    "Your supports and prices": the supports the person uses, the item each
    is claimed against and today's price limit, printed from the file on
    request (participant and admin). No click. Register row is live: held
    for everyone with a confirmed plan and a recorded funding arrangement.
    Earlier schedule acceptances remain as documents on the file.

  THE PRIVACY CONSENT IS CLAUSE 9 — reversed in v86.2.1, see above
    (the separate consent is a required click again)

  THE DOCUMENTS PAGE IS TIDIER
    One line per agreement, with the newest acceptance and the earlier
    editions folded underneath; acceptances no longer repeat in the
    documents list as "recorded, no file"; a "printed from your file" line
    links the supports-and-prices printout, the intake form and the
    emergency plan; the bookings-page card names what has been agreed and
    points to the account page for the editions.

  The Service Agreement's text is DMHC's substance in the platforms'
  structure, and a few positions in it are DMHC's to confirm with its
  legal reviewer before launch — the Privacy & terms page already says the
  legal wording is being settled: clause 10 (transport incidents not
  covered by insurance; excluded liability), clause 8.1 (grounds for
  suspension), and clause 8.2 (28 days' notice from us; immediate grounds).
  Change the text in serviceAgreementBody() in server.js and bump
  AGREEMENT_EDITION; every participant is then asked again.

  No migration. SCHEMA_VERSION stays 86000.


WHAT CHANGED IN v86.1.0 — THE SERVICE AGREEMENT IS NOT A FORM
-------------------------------------------------------------
The Service Agreement a participant was asked to agree to on screen was
the printed PDF: dotted lines for the name and address, a "fromdate"
placeholder, three signature blocks and two blank annexure tables. Pressing
"I agree" under a page of blanks and signature boxes was a contradiction.

  The agreement is now generated for the person, like the Schedule of
  Supports: DMHC's fifteen clauses in the same order, with the parties, the
  NDIS number, the funding arrangement (clause 9 states theirs), the
  representative and the cancellation windows filled in from the file, and
  the signature page replaced by the click record. The page is snapshotted
  beside the acceptance. Edition string: "service-agreement v2, on-screen
  edition, issued 22/08/2026".

  Anyone who agreed to the PDF sees "Service Agreement needs a fresh click"
  on their documents page, with the new one to read and an I agree button.
  The earlier acceptance is kept and still links to the archived PDF. Until
  they click, nothing about their supports changes and the booking gate
  still passes on the earlier acceptance.

  The PDF stays on the blank-forms shelf as the text of earlier acceptances.
  Uploading a new version of it no longer changes what anyone is asked to
  agree to — a generated document wins over a shelf copy for every key that
  has one (agreement, schedule, medication consent). To change the
  agreement's wording, edit GENERATED_DOCS['p-agreement'] in server.js and
  bump AGREEMENT_EDITION; every participant is then asked again.

  Wording changes from the PDF, for the record: the complaints clause gives
  hello@bookit.life, the phone number and the Contact page (the PDF gave a
  personal Gmail address and "by post to" a web address); the cancellation
  clause states the windows the platform actually publishes (7 days for
  personal care, daily tasks and community; 48 hours for employment,
  household and transport) instead of a flat seven days; clause 4 no longer
  refers to an intake form, because there isn't one. Nothing else changed
  in substance. Read it once before Monday.

  No migration. SCHEMA_VERSION stays 86000.


WHAT CHANGED IN v86.0.0 — THE PARTICIPANT'S FILE
------------------------------------------------
A participant should be asked for the facts about them as fields, for the
agreement on screen, and then only for the clinical plans their support
needs. DMHC's file asked for the same things as sixteen documents. v86
keeps the office file the same size and makes the participant's page
honest.

  DERIVED FROM DATA (no longer on anyone's to-do list; printed on demand)
    Intake form           from the account, billing card, nominee card and
                          the confirmed support plan
    Emergency & disaster  from the plan's emergency, continuity and safety
    plan                  sections
    Annual review         = plan confirmed within 12 months + every clinical
                          plan that applies on file and in date
    Advocate / nominee    = "who manages this account" on the documents page
                          (role, name, relationship, paid or not, under 18).
                          Under 18 with nobody named blocks the first
                          booking. A worker who takes the
                          person's shifts cannot be named. Signed form still
                          on the shelf, optional, for arrangements that need
                          a signature.
    NDIS plan dates       on the billing card (start/end). A plan that has
                          ended while supports continue is named in the
                          register that day. The plan COPY is now optional.
    Satisfaction survey   sent from the admin board, answered in BookIt,
                          anonymous if the person wants, tracked as
                          sent/answered, exported as 05a in the audit pack.
                          Never counted against the participant.

  GENERATED AND ACCEPTED BY CLICK
    Schedule of Supports  rendered from the certificate and the 2026-27
                          price limits for the supports the person uses.
                          The click snapshots the page beside the
                          acceptance. When the limits move, the file shows
                          it and asks for a fresh click — old acceptances
                          are kept.
    Medication consent    DMHC's own wording, split from the prescriber's
                          plan, accepted on screen.

  THE SUPPORT PLAN ASKS SEVEN YES/NO QUESTIONS ("Specialised support")
    medication · mealtimes · seizures · diabetes · allergy/asthma plan ·
    transfers · behaviour support plan. A yes switches on the matching
    clinical document (epilepsy, diabetes, allergy, manual handling and PBS
    rows are new; medication and mealtime gained the switch); a no means
    the row is never asked for. Plans confirmed before v86 read "not asked"
    — the page shows the only-if hints exactly as before until the person
    reviews their plan. Also added to the plan: living situation (required),
    diagnosis (optional), interpreter (required).

  WORKERS CAN READ THE CLINICAL PLANS
    A worker who is requested, booked or has worked with the participant
    can open the office-checked clinical plans (and only those) before the
    shift; the brief links them and every opening lands in the
    participant's access log. The participant can switch sharing off, but
    only with a written line saying how the worker gets the plan on the day
    Nothing else on the file is ever readable by a worker.

  SMALLER
    Privacy consent re-asked on change, not yearly. Medication plan on a
    12-month clock (was on-change). Risk assessment shown to the participant
    as status only ("on file, done 3/3/26" / "not written yet"). A checked
    clinical plan ticks the matching care plan the participant named. ATSI
    status and preferred contact on Your details (optional). Forms register
    counts acceptances made on screen as held — clicked agreements used to
    show as "nothing on file" in the register, which was a bug.
    Participant register CSV gained funding, plan dates, nominee, under-18
    and sharing columns.

  NOT BUILT, ON PURPOSE
    No booking gate on high-intensity plans. DMHC does not hold any
    Schedule 2 support and is not offering high-intensity supports yet.
    No date of birth — the account page promises not to ask for one.

  MIGRATION
    Columns are added by runLegacyAlter on first boot; nothing is
    backfilled. Two new tables: survey_invites, survey_responses. Existing
    form_templates rows for p-intake and p-emergency stay in the database
    but no longer appear anywhere, because those keys left the catalogue.
    SCHEMA_VERSION 86000.


WHAT THIS REPOSITORY IS
-----------------------
One Node.js program and one web page, with three small support modules.
No build step, no framework, no second service to keep in sync.

  server.js                  the entire back end
  lib/                       REQUIRED — server.js loads these on its first
                             three lines and will not start without them
    bookit-hardening.js        security helpers (uploads, CSV, origin checks,
                               static-path denial, production preflight)
    migration-runner.js        database migration helpers
    version.js                 reads package.json — so package.json is also
                               required at runtime, not just for npm
  public/index.html          the entire front end
  public/assets/             videos, photographs, character files, and two
                             browser scripts (dialog focus, worker-list guard)
  public/vendor/             the character/animation engine
  package.json               required at runtime by lib/version.js
  package-lock.json          lets `npm ci` work
  .gitignore                 keeps the database, documents, photos and the
                             signing key out of this PUBLIC repository


DEPLOYING A NEW VERSION
-----------------------
THE OLD "just upload server.js and public/" INSTRUCTION IS NO LONGER SAFE.
Since v85.2.0 the server requires lib/. Upload without it and the service
dies immediately with a module-not-found error.

The minimum deployable set is:

  server.js
  package.json
  package-lock.json
  lib/                       all three files
  public/index.html
  public/assets/bookit-a11y-dialogs.js
  public/assets/bookit-worker-load-guard.js

Videos, photographs, character files and public/vendor/ are unchanged by a
code release and do NOT need re-uploading. GitHub's upload page adds and
overwrites; it never deletes what you leave out.

Steps:

  1. Note the commit you are on now, so there is a rollback point:
         git -C <app dir> rev-parse --short HEAD

  2. Back up the three things a redeploy cannot recreate. Confirm where they
     actually live first — do not assume:
         sudo systemctl show bookit -p Environment -p WorkingDirectory -p ExecStart
     DB_PATH, DOCS_DIR and PHOTOS_DIR in that output are the truth. If unset,
     the database sits beside server.js wherever ExecStart points.

  3. Upload to github.com/thapasupreeya3-cpu/bookit-app/upload/main, commit.

     Uploading a DOTFILE (.gitignore, .github/): macOS Finder hides files
     beginning with a dot, so dragging a folder silently skips them. Press
     Shift + Command + . to reveal them first, or create the file directly
     with github.com/thapasupreeya3-cpu/bookit-app/new/main

  4. On the server:  sudo bookit-update

  5. Verify, in this order:
         /api/version                 reports the version you just pushed
         open one service page        the scene video plays through to its
                                      ending photograph
         log in                       (you will re-authenticate once)
         open the admin boards        they load
         download one CSV export      it opens

  6. If anything is wrong: check out the commit from step 1 and run
     sudo bookit-update again.

Verify an upload against raw.githubusercontent.com, never GitHub's file
page. The blob page caches its line count for a long time and has already
once shown a stale figure that read as a failed upload when it was fine.


BEFORE ANY DEPLOY
-----------------
  npm ci --ignore-scripts
  npm run check

Both must exit 0.

Be clear about what that currently proves: `npm run check` runs SYNTAX
VALIDATION ONLY on server.js, the three lib/ files and the two browser
scripts. It is not a test suite, not a security scan, and not a browser
regression run. The v85.3.0 package contains tests/, scripts/, ops/ and a
GitHub Actions workflow that would make it a real gate, but those were
deliberately not uploaded to keep the release minimal.

IF YOU LATER UPLOAD tests/, scripts/, ops/ AND .github/, replace
package.json AT THE SAME TIME with the fuller version from the v85.3.0
package. The two halves must travel together — uploading the fuller
package.json without the folders is what previously left the repository
advertising commands it could not run.


ENVIRONMENT SETTINGS WORTH KNOWING
----------------------------------
Full list and defaults are in the site guide; these three are the ones that
decide whether the server starts at all.

  NODE_ENV=production   turns on a fail-closed preflight. With it set, the
  or STRICT_PROD=on     server REFUSES TO START unless APP_URL is https, a
                        persistent secret exists, admin MFA is not disabled,
                        SEED_DEMO is not on, a mail provider is configured,
                        BIND_HOST is loopback, and the data folders sit
                        outside public/. It names the reason and touches
                        nothing before deciding. If both NODE_ENV=production
                        and SEED_DEMO=on are set, the boot will fail.

  BIND_HOST             defaults to 127.0.0.1, so port 3000 is unreachable
                        from the internet even if a firewall rule goes
                        missing. Caddy is the only intended caller.

  TRUST_PROXY=1         required for per-visitor rate limiting. Without it
                        every visitor shares one bucket, because behind
                        Caddy they all arrive from 127.0.0.1.


CURRENT STATE — HONEST NOTES
----------------------------
* Video SEEKING still jumps back to the start. The server does not serve
  byte ranges. Normal playback is unaffected; only dragging the scrubber.

* There is no administrator idle timeout. Sessions run 30 days as before.
  Deliberately deferred so a sole administrator cannot be locked out.

* The numbered migration ledger is NOT active. lib/migration-runner.js
  contains ensureMigrationTable() and runMigrations(), but server.js calls
  neither — it uses runLegacyAlter() only. What IS true: unexpected
  migration errors are now rethrown instead of being silently swallowed.
  Do not describe this as a completed versioned-migration system.

* package.json is named "bookit-site" while package-lock.json still says
  "bookit-site-v65". Cosmetic only; npm ci works. Regenerating the lock
  would tidy it.

* No test suite, CI workflow, backup tooling or operations documentation is
  on this branch. That is a deliberate minimal-release choice, not an
  oversight. They exist in the full v85.3.0 package.
