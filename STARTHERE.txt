WHAT CHANGED IN v86.9.0 — THE GROWTH RELEASE
--------------------------------------------
  Everything from the 30 August profitability and design review, plus
  inactive night care done properly. One migration: ten nullable columns on
  bookings and a new referrals table (SCHEMA_VERSION 86900). 262 routes,
  48 tables. Full upload of the changed files, then sudo bookit-update.

  INACTIVE NIGHT CARE (SLEEPOVERS), DONE PROPERLY
    The NDIS Night-Time Sleepover item (01_010_0107 / 01_832_0115, 11.79
    in 2026-27) is one flat price for an eight-hour night with the worker
    asleep on the premises, and it INCLUDES up to two hours of active
    support. That rule is now in the code: a sleepover is 8 hours (up to 10)
    starting between 8pm and 1am; when the worker closes the shift they
    record the active hours (0 if not woken); anything beyond two becomes
    its own line at the night rate for that day (weekday night, Saturday,
    Sunday), on the invoice, on the statement, in the PACE claim file (a
    second row, reference BKnnA) and in the worker's pay at their ladder
    share. New columns: active_hours, active_extra_hours/_category/_item/
    _rate/_total/_share, active_note. A new public page,
    /services/overnight, explains sleepovers versus active overnight in
    plain words, with the live price. Check SLEEPOVER_HOURS_MIN/MAX and
    SLEEPOVER_INCLUDED_ACTIVE_HOURS against each July's Pricing
    Arrangements.

  MEET-AND-GREET BEFORE THE PAPERWORK — FIFTEEN MINUTES, NOT A SHIFT
    An intro request (kind = 'intro', 0.25 h) needs only a confirmed email
    and a funding lane; the rest of the first-booking gate applies to the
    first real shift. It is a conversation — a video call or a quick hello
    — not a shift: no support is given, nobody is charged and nobody is
    paid, the same way an interview is not a shift. The rule that keeps
    that honest is the half hour: if it runs past thirty minutes it has
    become support, and the participant books a shift so the worker is
    paid. The worker closes it with one tap (no shift note needed). It is
    never on an invoice, a statement, a claim or the pay run (billable()
    and payable() both exclude it). If the office ever decides to pay for
    intros anyway, intro_paid_hours (setting, default 0) puts them on the
    pay run as not claimable. The KPI board counts intros held and how
    many led to a booking. It never repeats and counts for nothing on the
    ladder.

  THE ESTABLISHMENT FEE — WHO PAYS, AND WHEN IT MAY BE RAISED
    The participant's plan pays it, once, from Core ($735.80 in 2026-27).
    The Pricing Arrangements attach conditions, and the schedule you have
    carries the price only, so read the Arrangements before raising one.
    In outline: the participant is new to the provider; they are starting
    a substantial ongoing program of personal care or participation (the
    Arrangements name 20 hours a month for at least three months); the fee
    is in the signed service agreement; once per participant. And, our
    own rule on top: only where the set-up was real, documented work — a
    behaviour support plan, restrictive practices, a complex handover —
    never for a routine sign-up. The tick on Growth & money says exactly
    that, and the refusal repeats it. Non-face-to-face time is for writing
    plans and reports, not for phone calls, which are part of the service.

  THE DIRECTORY SAYS WHEN
    Every worker card shows "Next free: Tue 9am" — the first two-hour
    daytime slot in the next fortnight their weekday pattern allows and the
    diary does not hold. An estimate for a card; the booking form still asks.

  THE OPEN-SHIFT FEED (#/shifts)
    Every cover request the cascade has open, shown to any signed-in worker
    who is eligible for it (service, day, credentials, diary), with what they
    would be paid. "Take this shift" runs the same gates as an offer and
    tells the participant. Workers see it in the nav.

  WORKER REFERRALS
    Each worker has a code (W<id>-XXXXXX, on My earnings) and a link; a new
    worker types it at sign-up. Once the new worker has completed
    referral_qualify_hours (setting, default 50) the referrer is owed
    referral_bonus (setting, default 50), notified, and the office marks
    it paid from Admin › Growth & money. Participants are never offered
    anything for a referral (Code of Conduct).
    HOW IT WORKS, MECHANICALLY: the code is HMAC(secret, worker id), so it
    cannot be guessed and needs no table. At sign-up, POST /api/register
    with ref=<code> writes one referrals row (referrer, referee, code,
    created). Nothing else happens until the referred worker's completed
    ordinary shifts (not sleepovers, not intros) reach the mark. The
    nightly job 'referrals' (and the office opening the referrals list)
    runs reviewReferrals(): it sets qualified_at, hours_at_qualify and
    amount, and emails the referrer. From then until it is marked paid the
    bonus is a row on /api/admin/payroll.csv ("Referral bonus", not
    claimable) beside the shifts, and shows as payable on the worker's My
    earnings and on /refer-a-worker. The office pays it through the normal
    pay run and presses Mark paid; that writes paid_at and paid_by, and the
    row leaves the payroll file. The bonus is the provider's cost — it is
    never on a participant's invoice or in a claim. Public page:
    /refer-a-worker (footer, Work with us, and the worker's account menu).

  THE ACCOUNT MENU HAS DOORS FOR WORKERS
    Open shifts, My earnings, The ladder, Refer a worker, and Pay rates &
    working with us (the public Work with us page) — a signed-in worker
    could not previously reach the public worker pages without the footer.

  THE PRICE LIST IS COMPLETE
    The public rate table (/api/rates › rates, the pricing page's first
    table) now has the sleepover row, per night; the claim table has rows
    for the sleepover and active-overnight items; a second table,
    "Everything else on the price list", is rendered from /api/rates ›
    extras: meet-and-greet, sleepover with the included hours and the
    extra-active rates, active overnight, travel per km, short-notice
    cancellation with every window, the establishment fee, non-face-to-face
    time, group shifts, and the no-fees line.

  ADMIN › GROWTH & MONEY
    Ten KPIs with their definitions (fill rate, utilisation, cancellations
    and how many short-notice ones went unbilled, uncovered shifts, days to
    cash by funding lane, margin per hour by category, sign-up → first
    booking, 90-day retention, worker churn, time to verify). Referrals.
    Concierge onboarding: open a participant account on the phone with the
    consent recorded in words; they set their own password from the email.
    One-off claimable lines: the establishment fee (35.80, once per
    participant, you tick that the conditions are met) and non-face-to-face
    time (writing a plan; the weekday rate, the same item as the support).
    Group shifts: link two or three shifts (same worker, date, start,
    length) as 1:2 or 1:3 — each participant pays the price ÷ ratio, the
    worker is paid once — OFF until the setting group_supports=on, because
    it claims the 0136 group items and that is its own registration.

  COORDINATOR REFERRALS
    A form on /for-plan-managers (POST /api/referrals, public, rate
    limited): the coordinator gets a reference and a promise in writing —
    a reply within two business hours — and every admin is notified.

  SUBURB PAGES
    /support-workers-in/<suburb> for every suburb with a visible worker:
    counts and services, no names, a button into the filtered directory;
    all in the sitemap. Generated from the profiles, so they stay true.

  HOW THE PARTICIPANT IS TOLD TO PAY (found on the first trial shift)
    Before this release an invoice only existed once the office pressed
    Run on the Claims board, and the participant had no prompt at all —
    they saw a statement (a list of shifts) and a CSV, neither of which is
    an invoice. Now:
    1. The night a shift is approved (by the participant, or deemed on day
       7), the job 'invoices' runs the self- and plan-managed lanes of the
       claims run: one invoice per participant per run, numbered
       INV-YYYYMMDD-<id>, stamped on every booking in it. The run is now
       gated on approval_state = 'approved' — it never was before, so a
       queried shift could have been invoiced. Agency lines still wait for
       the office (the PACE file has to be uploaded by a person).
    2. The participant (self-managed) is emailed the PDF with a Pay by card
       button (Stripe, when STRIPE_SECRET_KEY is set) and gets a message in
       their account; a plan-managed invoice goes to the plan manager's
       email and the participant is told it went there.
    3. In the account: a red banner at the top of Bookings ("An invoice to
       pay — $X, due dd/mm; see the invoice, the PDF and how to pay") and
       the Invoices table at the top of Statements & invoices: number,
       date, due, lines, total, status (due / overdue / paid), PDF, Pay by
       card. The statement below it now says what it is — every shift with
       its note and cost — and that the invoice is what gets paid.
    4. The PDF is a proper tax invoice, modelled on the office's own:
       company name, ABN, address (COMPANY_ADDRESS, default 16 Crystal
       Crescent, Wyong NSW 2259), NDIS registration number, email, phone;
       invoice number, date, DUE DATE (invoice_due_days, default 14); bill
       to — name, suburb, email, phone, NDIS number (or the plan manager);
       one line per support with the NDIS item's own name ("Assistance
       With Self-Care Activities - Standard - Sunday"), the item number,
       the time range and hours (or the night, or the kilometres), rate
       and amount; invoice total, paid, BALANCE DUE; payment block with
       account name, BSB 067-873 and account number 2577 2190 (the code's
       default; BANK_DETAILS in /etc/bookit.env overrides it), the
       invoice number as the reference; the GST-free statement; page
       numbers when it runs long. ITEM_NAMES holds the 33 item names from
       the 2026-27 schedule; regenerate when the schedule changes.
    5. Paying: a card payment marks the invoice paid through the Stripe
       webhook, as before. A bank transfer is marked by the office — a new
       "Invoice paid" button on each row of the Claims board marks the
       whole invoice at once (POST /api/admin/invoices/:no/paid), logged
       to the compliance log; the participant's banner clears.
    GET /api/me/invoices and GET /api/me/invoices/:no.pdf are the
    participant's (and their coordinator's, with the invoices scope); the
    PDF is generated from the bookings each time, so it is always the
    current state, and a worker or another participant gets a 403.

  REMOVING RECORDS — ERASE, VOID, CLOSE (with a typed reason)
    A registered provider keeps the records of the supports it delivered,
    so "delete" is two different things and the office is asked which.
    Every action needs a typed reason of at least twenty characters, goes
    to the compliance log, and the erased rows are copied to a new table,
    erasures (kind, ref, label, snapshot, reason, by, at), so the audit
    trail still knows they existed.
    WHERE THE BUTTONS ARE (account menu › Admin):
      Bookings & rosters › Recent bookings — "Remove…" on any shift that
        is not on a claim: requested, accepted, cancelled or completed.
      Claims — "Remove…" on an unclaimed line; "Withdraw invoice…" on a
        line that carries an invoice number (takes the whole invoice
        back; every line returns to unclaimed, the participant or plan
        manager is emailed not to pay it, the card link dies); "Unclaim…"
        on an agency line (no invoice number). A paid invoice cannot be
        withdrawn — mark it unpaid first if that was a mistake.
      Participant files — "Close account…" on each participant card.
      Workers — "Close account…" on each applicant card.
      People — "Close…" on any row that is not an admin, not you and not
        a demo account.
    THE ORDER, for something that has already been invoiced: Claims ›
    Withdraw invoice… (reason) → the shift is unclaimed → Remove… › Erase
    (reason, and the "this was a test" tick if it was delivered) → then
    Close account… on the participant if the account was only a trial.
    SHIFTS — "Remove…" opens the dialog:
      Void  — a delivered shift the office wants out of everything but
              which must stay on file: it becomes a cancellation that is
              never short-notice with voided = 1, total and worker share
              zeroed; billable(), payable(), the diary, the ladder and the
              statement all ignore it. (The status column's CHECK allows
              only the five working states, hence the flag.)
      Erase — the row is deleted, with its notes, reviews and cover rows.
              Only for something that was never a real support: a trial,
              a test, an undelivered booking. Erasing a DELIVERED shift is
              refused unless the "this was a test" box is ticked.
      A claimed or paid line is refused either way: take it off the claim
      first (Claims board › unpaid), then remove it.
    ACCOUNTS — "Close account…" on a participant card (Participant files)
    or a worker card (the applicant list). If the person never had a
    delivered shift the account and everything attached to it is erased
    (bookings, documents on disk, photo, sessions, links, messages). If
    they did, the office is asked to acknowledge, and the account is
    closed and de-identified: name → "Removed participant #id", email →
    removed-id@invalid.bookit.life, phone, suburb, NDIS number, nominee
    and plan-manager details cleared, password rotated, verified off,
    closed_at stamped (new column), photo and documents deleted from
    disk, open bookings cancelled; the delivered shifts, notes, invoices
    and incidents stay, which is what the retention rules want. Admin
    accounts are refused (revoke admin first); your own account is
    refused from here.
    Routes: DELETE /api/admin/bookings/:id (reason, test), POST
    /api/admin/bookings/:id/void (reason), POST
    /api/admin/invoices/:no/withdraw (reason), POST
    /api/admin/claims/:id/unclaim (reason), DELETE /api/admin/users/:id
    (reason, acknowledge), GET /api/admin/erasures. Nothing here is
    reachable from the participant or worker side; "Remove" on a filed
    document (Participant files) is the older, separate action.
    FOR YOUR TRIAL: the trial shift is unclaimed, so Remove… › Erase with
    the "this was a test" tick, reason "trial shift used to test the
    site", removes it and its note; the trial participant's account, if
    it has no other delivered shift, can then be closed and is erased.

  THE KILOMETRES ARE CLAIMED NOW
    Provider travel entered by the worker at the end of a shift was charged
    on the participant's statement and nowhere else — never on the invoice,
    never in the PACE claim file. It now appears on the invoice as a
    "Provider travel — non-labour costs" line and in the claim file as its
    own row (reference BKnnT) against the $1.00 non-labour travel item for
    the support's registration group (TRAVEL_ITEMS), quantity = dollars.
    Cancelled shifts carry no travel. Before this release, every kilometre
    since travel was introduced was revenue left on the table; the old
    statements are still right, the claim file simply omitted the row.

WHAT CHANGED IN v86.8.0 — THE REVIEW RELEASE
--------------------------------------------
  Everything from the 29 August review, in one release. No migration:
  SCHEMA_VERSION stays 86400 and nothing is added to the database. Server,
  page, scripts and docs all change, so this is a FULL upload (see the
  deploy list below) followed by sudo bookit-update.

  THE DOOR IS SHUT BY DEFAULT
    The dispatcher now refuses every /api/ route without a session and every
    /api/admin/ route without the office BEFORE the handler runs. The public
    routes are an explicit list, PUBLIC_API in server.js (21 of them: the
    directory, rates, scope, the sign-in family, contact, the public jobs
    board, the invite reader). Every handler keeps its own check as well.
    Two routes that answered a 500 when logged out (/api/me/blockers and
    /api/admin/participants/:id/plan-review) now answer 401/403.

  AN ASYNC HANDLER CAN NO LONGER STOP THE PROCESS
    Three handlers are async (email test, claims run, AI backfill). A
    rejection inside one used to escape the dispatcher's try/catch as an
    unhandled rejection, which on Node 22 ends the process. Promises are
    caught now, the same as thrown errors.

  ONE WORKER, ONE PLACE AT A TIME
    bookingClash() beside bookingStart(). A booking request is refused
    against the worker's accepted shifts and against the participant's own
    overlapping request; an accept is refused against the worker's other
    accepted shifts; a moved occurrence and a series edit are refused the
    same way, and a series edit that clashes changes nothing and names the
    dates. The window is derived from the shift's interval, so both sides
    of midnight are read (a 22:00 sleepover tonight against an 01:00 shift
    already accepted for tomorrow, and the reverse). workerFree(), which the
    cover cascade, the candidate lists, the admin cover assignment and the
    office assignment all ask through workerEligible(), now delegates to
    the same helper (it used to read the shift's own calendar date only).
    tests/clash-tests.js has twenty-one cases; tests/smoke.js drives the
    office assignment across midnight in both directions.

  SERIES WRITES ARE ONE WRITE
    Creating, editing and ending a repeating booking each run inside
    BEGIN IMMEDIATE … COMMIT. A failure on the ninth occurrence leaves no
    rule and no occurrences, not a rule with eight.

  AN OFFICE SESSION THAT IS NOT USED FOR 12 HOURS IS OVER
    ADMIN_IDLE_HOURS (default 12), admin accounts only. Participants and
    workers keep the thirty-day cookie. Signing back in is the normal door
    with the second step; nobody is locked out. This closes the "no
    administrator idle timeout" note below.

  A FIRST BOOKING NEEDS A CONFIRMED EMAIL ADDRESS
    "Confirm your email address" joins the first-booking blockers (red,
    theirs, with the resend link at the top of the page). The account still
    works unverified — browsing, messaging, the plan — only the first shift
    waits. Demo accounts are exempt.

  A VISITOR SEES "ZOE T."
    /api/workers and /api/workers/:id give the short name to anyone without
    a session, the full name to a member. Photo and suburb are unchanged.
    PUBLIC_WORKER_NAMES=full restores the old behaviour.

  THE PUBLIC PAGES EXIST FOR SEARCH ENGINES
    The 27 public pages (PUBLIC_PAGES in server.js) are served at their real
    paths — https://bookit.life/services/transport is a 200 with its own
    <title>, description, canonical link and og:url written into the <head>
    the server sends; the home page carries Organization JSON-LD. The page
    then folds the path into the hash on load (route() in index.html), so
    the app runs exactly as before. /sitemap.xml lists them; /robots.txt
    allows the site and names the sitemap (the SITE_PASSWORD gate still
    answers Disallow while it is on). Account, admin and worker paths still
    redirect into the hash and are never indexed.

  STATIC FILES: STREAMED, RANGED, COMPRESSED, REVALIDATED
    serveStatic() streams from disk instead of reading whole files into
    memory; videos answer Range requests (206), so seeking works — this
    closes the "video seeking jumps back to the start" note below; text is
    brotli/gzip compressed once per deploy and kept in memory (the 1 MB page
    is 224 KB over the wire); every file carries an ETag, so an unchanged
    page is a 304. The page's Cache-Control is now no-cache (revalidate,
    then 304) rather than no-store. Caddy's own encode setting, if on, is
    harmless: an already-encoded response passes through.

  FONTS ARE SELF-HOSTED
    Sora, Inter and Fraunces from public/assets/fonts/ (SIL OFL, from the
    Fontsource packages, licences beside them), same family names, so the
    stylesheet did not change. The Google origins are gone from the page
    and from the CSP, which now names no other origin at all.

  BACKUPS ARE IN THE REPOSITORY
    scripts/backup.js (VACUUM INTO for a consistent copy of a live WAL
    database, integrity-checked; both folders archived and checked against
    the archive; prune; optional S3/Lightsail-bucket sync; exits non-zero
    on failure; exits 2 — INCOMPLETE, the timer shows red — when a row in
    the snapshot refers to a document or photo that is not in the archive,
    after still writing and copying the set) and ops/bookit-backup.service +
    .timer (between 02:15 and 02:25 Sydney time, using /etc/bookit.env).
    Setting them up is in ops/README.md. The old "cp the .db file" procedure
    must not be used on a running site.

  THE GUIDE'S PROMISES ARE KEPT
    docs/api-route-inventory.txt and docs/database-table-inventory.txt are
    generated by scripts/inventory.js and checked by npm run check. The
    table inventory is read back with PRAGMA table_info from a database the
    server itself created and migrated on a throwaway file, so it is what a
    fresh bookit.db actually contains, column for column. .env.example lists
    every setting the code reads, with its default.
    scripts/dbq.js is the read-only query helper (the guide called it
    tests/dbq.js). tests/ holds the clash tests and a smoke test that boots
    the server on a throwaway database; .github/workflows/check.yml runs
    npm run check on every push. npm run check is now: syntax, inventories
    up to date, clash tests, smoke test.

  SMALLER
    og:image and twitter:image are absolute URLs (link previews were blank).
    X-Frame-Options is DENY everywhere (it was SAMEORIGIN on static files).
    The boot log names the two blank forms it skips on purpose (p-intake,
    p-emergency — both generated from the file since v86.0.0). The silent
    catches around boot fix-ups and sign-out-everywhere now log a warning;
    a failed plan-acknowledgement write refuses the accept instead of
    accepting on a tick nobody can find later. package-lock.json records
    the real version. engines is >=22.12 <23 and .nvmrc names the exact
    release (22.23.2) that CI runs: node:sqlite is experimental on Node 22
    and its API moves between majors. Make the box match .nvmrc (or change
    .nvmrc to what the box runs) and treat an upgrade as a change to test.

  NOT DONE, ON PURPOSE
    Splitting the page's JavaScript so participants do not download the
    admin boards (a larger refactor, planned). Deleting the unreferenced
    public/vendor/ and care-cast/ assets (confirm with a browser network
    trace first; a GitHub upload never deletes anyway). A series edit that
    changes the time or the worker still leaves occurrences "accepted" —
    whether they should go back to "requested" is a product decision.

WHAT CHANGED IN v86.6.3 — A ROW THAT HINGES ON THE PLAN WAITS FOR THE PLAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  With no support plan on the account (a brand-new person, or the test
  account after a full clear), every "only if the plan says so" row —
  medication consent, medication plan, mealtime, epilepsy, diabetes,
  allergy, transfers, behaviour support — was landing in the red
  "before your first booking" box, because an unanswered question was

  which is where boxes belong.

DEPLOYING A NEW VERSION
-----------------------
THE OLD "just upload server.js and public/" INSTRUCTION IS NO LONGER SAFE.
Since v85.2.0 the server requires lib/. Upload without it and the service
dies immediately with a module-not-found error.

The minimum deployable set is:

  server.js
  package.json
  package-lock.json
  .nvmrc                     since v86.8.0 — the exact Node release
  lib/                       all three files
  public/index.html
  public/assets/bookit-a11y-dialogs.js
  public/assets/bookit-worker-load-guard.js
  public/assets/fonts/       since v86.8.0 — the page loads its fonts from here
  scripts/  tests/  docs/    since v86.8.0 — npm run check needs all three

Videos, photographs, character files and public/vendor/ are unchanged by a
code release and do NOT need re-uploading. GitHub's upload page adds and
overwrites; it never deletes what you leave out.

Steps:

  1. Note the commit you are on now, so there is a rollback point:
         git -C <app dir> rev-parse --short HEAD

  2. Back up the three things a redeploy cannot recreate. Confirm where they
     actually live first — do not assume:
         sudo systemctl show bookit -p Environment -p WorkingDirectory -p ExecStart
     DB_PATH, DOCS_DIR and PHOTOS_DIR in that output are the truth. If unset,
     the database sits beside server.js wherever ExecStart points.

  3. Upload to github.com/thapasupreeya3-cpu/bookit-app/upload/main, commit.

     Uploading a DOTFILE (.gitignore, .github/): macOS Finder hides files
     beginning with a dot, so dragging a folder silently skips them. Press
     Shift + Command + . to reveal them first, or create the file directly
     with github.com/thapasupreeya3-cpu/bookit-app/new/main

  4. On the server:  sudo bookit-update

  5. Verify, in this order:
         /api/version                 reports the version you just pushed
         open one service page        the scene video plays through to its
                                      ending photograph
         log in                       (you will re-authenticate once)
         open the admin boards        they load
         download one CSV export      it opens

  6. If anything is wrong: check out the commit from step 1 and run
     sudo bookit-update again.

Verify an upload against raw.githubusercontent.com, never GitHub's file
page. The blob page caches its line count for a long time and has already
once shown a stale figure that read as a failed upload when it was fine.

ENVIRONMENT SETTINGS WORTH KNOWING
----------------------------------
Full list and defaults are in the site guide; these three are the ones that
decide whether the server starts at all.

  NODE_ENV=production   turns on a fail-closed preflight. With it set, the
  or STRICT_PROD=on     server REFUSES TO START unless APP_URL is https, a
                        persistent secret exists, admin MFA is not disabled,
                        SEED_DEMO is not on, a mail provider is configured,
                        BIND_HOST is loopback, and the data folders sit
                        outside public/. It names the reason and touches
                        nothing before deciding. If both NODE_ENV=production
                        and SEED_DEMO=on are set, the boot will fail.

  BIND_HOST             defaults to 127.0.0.1, so port 3000 is unreachable
                        from the internet even if a firewall rule goes
                        missing. Caddy is the only intended caller.

  TRUST_PROXY=1         required for per-visitor rate limiting. Without it
                        every visitor shares one bucket, because behind
                        Caddy they all arrive from 127.0.0.1.


CURRENT STATE — HONEST NOTES
----------------------------
* Video seeking works since v86.8.0 (byte ranges). Admin sessions time out
  after 12 idle hours since v86.8.0. Both earlier notes are closed.

* The numbered migration ledger is NOT active. lib/migration-runner.js
  contains ensureMigrationTable() and runMigrations(), but server.js calls
  neither — it uses runLegacyAlter() only. What IS true: unexpected
  migration errors are rethrown instead of being silently swallowed.
  Do not describe this as a completed versioned-migration system.

* Both package.json and package-lock.json are named "bookit-site-v65"; the
  lock records the same version as package.json since v86.8.0. Regenerate
  the lock (or edit the two version fields) on every release.

* Tests, a CI workflow, backup tooling and operations notes are on this
  branch since v86.8.0 (tests/, .github/, scripts/backup.js, ops/). There
  is still no browser regression run and no security scan.

* Authentication is scryptSync: every sign-in blocks the event loop for
  roughly fifty milliseconds. Fine at today's scale; the first busy Monday
  morning is when it would show, and the fix (crypto.scrypt, async) is
  small now that the dispatcher handles promises.
