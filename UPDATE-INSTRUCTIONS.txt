   preserving paths and replacing matching files. Commit the changes.
   Upload all supplied files, including both new lib/care-routine-*.js files.
   This is a partial update, so keep other existing repository files.
3. Confirm the new GitHub Actions check succeeds.
4. On your EXISTING Lightsail bookit instance, open Connect using SSH and run:
      sudo bookit-update
5. Refresh the website. /api/version should report 88.4.17.
   The default schema identifier is 88408.
6. Open Bookings > Care routine > Add regular shift.
   Choose Every week or Every fortnight, then Ongoing or On a specific date.
   Review the first dates and prices before sending the request.

Future requests are added automatically as they enter the next eight weeks.
There is no limit on the routine's total number of visits. An end-date routine
stops at the selected date; an ongoing routine continues until you end it.
Each request still needs the worker's acceptance. Totals shown in the preview
cover the displayed dates only. Later visits get their applicable dated prices.
If a future date cannot be requested, check the selected routine and Next actions.

No new hosting settings or service credentials are required for this feature.
The migration adds two routine tables and three series fields automatically.
Existing finite routines keep their original end dates. Historical booking
prices, invoices and uploaded files are preserved. Keep your normal backup and
the live database, uploads and protected hosting configuration in place.
