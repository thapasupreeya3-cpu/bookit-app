WHAT CHANGES
The calendar popup never excludes carers because of their area. It orders them:
matching areas first, then nearby areas by known geographic distance, then
unresolved areas. There is no distance cutoff. Recent carers lead within equal
location rankings. Distant and unknown areas stay available through Next carer.

The compact popup has a visible Location box for suburb or postcode. Name search
is under Filters & visit details. Location only changes priority; other filters
and availability still apply. Check this visit includes all areas while checking
schedule and conflicts. Confirm the actual address and any required travel in
the booking form. The user guide explains the changes.

The compact layout, ongoing recurrence, booking email fixes and improved
shift-note workspace remain included. No new provider configuration is needed.
This source package does not deploy the live website or send customer emails.
Retain your usual backup before deploying.
