THE CARE WEB v88.4.1 — CUMULATIVE CHANGED-FILES UPDATE

Apply over v88.3.4 or v88.4.0. This ZIP includes the payment update and the
participant-address, booking-location and worker-service-area changes.

1. Preserve a backup of your existing source, deployed database, uploads,
   photos and environment settings.
2. Extract the ZIP and MERGE its files into your existing repository root,
   replacing matching source files. Do not replace the repository with this
   partial ZIP and do not delete files absent from it.
3. Preserve live databases, document/photo folders, secrets and provider keys.
4. Use Node 22 (>=22.12 <23), install dependencies if required and redeploy.
5. Check /api/version: APP_VERSION 88.4.1, SCHEMA_VERSION 88401. If your host
   overrides SCHEMA_VERSION, update that override accordingly.
6. In Settings, open Address & arrival details. New bookings have a Where will
   support start? choice. Existing visits retain their own location and may
   show Confirm meeting place. Missing addresses do not block invoicing.
7. Validate a participant/helper save, worker acceptance and location update
   on your deployed site. Local browser rendering and real message delivery
   remain unverified.

Payment setup remains one-time environment configuration. See
  docs/PAYMENT-SETUP-v88.4.0.md
  docs/STRIPE-CONNECTION-STATUS.md
The connected Stripe account checked here is a sandbox with no registered
webhooks. No live payment activation, wage transfer or deployment is claimed.
