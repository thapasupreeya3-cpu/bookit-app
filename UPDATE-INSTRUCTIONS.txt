THE CARE WEB v88.4.14 — CURRENT UPDATE ONLY

REQUIRED BASE: v88.4.13 (worker task readiness update installed)
This ZIP contains only files changed for this update. Merge it into your
existing repository; it is not a complete replacement repository.

INSTALL ON YOUR EXISTING WEBSITE
1. Keep a backup of your source, database, uploads and settings.
2. Extract and MERGE this ZIP into your v88.4.13 repository, replacing matching
   files. Keep every other file and folder.
3. Redeploy using your existing process and Node 22 (>=22.12 <23).
4. Refresh using Cmd+Shift+R (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows).
5. /api/version should show APP_VERSION 88.4.14 and SCHEMA_VERSION 88405.
   Two small notification tables and their indexes/triggers are created at
   startup. No reset, manual data change or separate website is required.

WHAT CHANGED
A worker accepting a new booking creates a saved, green Booking accepted
notice for the participant and currently authorised helpers. View booking
opens the exact visit from Calendar, List or elsewhere on the site. Each
viewer dismisses their own notice. The visible site refreshes normally within
15 seconds. Existing booking emails continue separately. Previously accepted
historical bookings do not receive a mass announcement after installation.

The opening park scene and fictional Sarah M. / Shift confirmed cards are
removed. The six service clips and the useful homepage controls remain.
The guide explains the current 8 pm split quote and distinguishes evening
support from active overnight care. This update does not change rate tables,
pricing calculations, claim-item mappings, issued invoices or funding rules.
The identified overnight entitlement / item-classification review is not
implemented by this notification update.

CHECK ON YOUR EXISTING WEBSITE
After installation, keep a participant signed in while an assigned worker
accepts their requested visit. Check the green notice, View booking link and
Dismiss action. Calendar and List should both support the notice. A helper
needs current booking permission for the selected participant. Changing or
cancelling the visit removes its obsolete confirmation at the next refresh.
Use Admin > Today > Email delivery for the separately queued email status.
