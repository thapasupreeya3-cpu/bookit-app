Supported earlier source versions: 88.1.4, 88.1.5, 88.2.0, 88.2.1.
Includes all updates since your uploaded bookit-app-main (4).zip (v88.1.4).

This is an update package, not a complete application download.
It contains 90 new or changed project files. Unchanged images,
videos, fonts and other files are omitted.

1. Extract the ZIP on your computer. Do not upload the ZIP itself.
2. Open your existing GitHub repository root (where server.js, package.json,
   public and lib are located).
3. Upload/merge all extracted project files and folders at that root,
   preserving their paths and replacing matching files. Keep unchanged
   contents of existing folders. Do not delete folders first.
4. Review and commit the changes. UPDATE-INSTRUCTIONS.txt is optional to upload.
5. Follow STARTHERE.txt and docs/CHANGELOG-v88.2.2.md for deployment and setup.
   Preserve your runtime database, uploaded documents, photos and configuration.

Do not create an extra enclosing directory. The application still needs the
unchanged files already in your repository. Compare separately if your
repository has later changes that were not supplied in this conversation.
