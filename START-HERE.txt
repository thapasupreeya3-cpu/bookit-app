BOOKIT SITE v65 — animation composition & collision fixes
==========================================================
This folder is the entire site: v64 plus the animation overhaul.

What changed in v65
  • Homepage journey has a protected corridor: right half of the hero on
    desktop (the static illustration cedes its column; the floating cards
    stay), and a dedicated 300px stage below the CTAs on mobile. The route
    never runs beneath the headline, buttons or search field.
  • The pair and bench are larger (with a minimum on small screens), the
    route line is softer, the pergola now straddles the path as an arbor
    sized to clear the wheelchair — no more clipping at the frame edge.
  • The pair walks in from off-screen and fully exits off-screen before the
    loop resets. Nothing fades except footprints and wheel tracks.
  • One obstacle-aware navigation system (public/vendor/care-nav.js, NEW
    FILE) now plans every journey — homepage scenes and all service-page
    dioramas. Clearance covers the whole travelling body (wheelchair,
    carried plates, watering can), not just the character's centre.
  • Shared-living scene: the obstacle map is actually used — the walk to
    the chairs goes around the dining table, and the walkway clears the
    island and table by a full body width.
  • Homepage garden scene: the exit route no longer cuts through the second
    raised bed; stories scene: trees/flowers moved off the walked path.
  • The Build 24/25 full-screen walkers are removed entirely — they crossed
    live copy at arbitrary heights and kept running behind other pages.
    Paused scenes now do zero GPU work when their page is hidden.

  • Community scene: pair legs are navigator-solved around the pavilion
    posts, art station, basket and bench; the exit walkway clears the
    relocated garden planter. Service-page animation windows are smaller
    (560px, centred). Personal-care entry no longer crosses the bench.
  • Homepage: the route is a real footpath, the carer pushes from the
    middle of the chair, a neighbour sits on the bench, two friends chat
    under the pergola, and the "Shift confirmed" card moved off the route.

Deploy: open github.com/thapasupreeya3-cpu/bookit-app/upload/main
and drag BOTH items from inside this folder (server.js and the
public folder) into the page. Commit, then: sudo bookit-update
(public/vendor/care-nav.js is new — make sure it uploads too.)
