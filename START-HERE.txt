BOOKIT SITE v66 — character upgrade (the approved "polished stylised 3D" cast)
==============================================================================
This folder is the entire site: v65 plus the new characters. Only two files
changed — public/vendor/care-motion.js and public/vendor/care-core.local.js.

What changed in v66
  • Every animated character — the homepage journey pair, the bench
    neighbour, the pergola friends, the gardener, the mower worker, the
    story walker, and all six service-page dioramas — is rebuilt to the
    approved concept style: realistic proportions (smaller head, neck,
    shoulders, waist), warm faces (brows, eyes with a catchlight, nose, a
    real smile, blush), mitten hands, fuller hair, and legs that bend at
    the knee while walking and sitting.
  • Support workers now wear the uniform across every scene: teal polo,
    short sleeves, lanyard and ID badge — matching the service dioramas
    and the concept art.
  • Seated characters rest their hands in their lap and breathe instead of
    the old rowing-arm loop; the wheelchair rider on the homepage has her
    silver hair from the concept.
  • The bench neighbour actually sits ON the bench slats now (hips at seat
    height, feet to the ground) instead of sinking into the frame, and
    wears clearly-clothing colours (teal jumper, amber trousers).
  • No layout, route, timing or navigation logic was touched: same
    skeleton pivots, same scene anchors, same obstacle-aware paths, same
    reduced-motion still frame. The carer's hands still land on the
    wheelchair push handles; held props (hairbrush, towels, resume,
    watering can) still sit in the same hands.

Deploy: open github.com/thapasupreeya3-cpu/bookit-app/upload/main
and drag BOTH items from inside this folder (server.js and the
public folder) into the page. Commit, then: sudo bookit-update
(only public/vendor/care-motion.js and public/vendor/care-core.local.js
actually changed since v65 — but dragging both items as usual is fine.)
