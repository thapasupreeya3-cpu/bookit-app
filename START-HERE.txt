BOOKIT SITE v65 — complete graphics upgrade
===========================
This folder is the entire BookIt site. Version 65 contains the calmer
homepage, one contained hero animation, six distinct service visual themes,
responsive AVIF/WebP photography, a branded social card, opt-in service scene
previews with Play/Pause/Replay/chapter controls, reduced-motion and WebGL
fallbacks, safer animation routes, and improved static-file delivery.

Important visual notes:
  - Worker profiles and testimonials in this preview are labelled illustrative.
  - No founder, team or worker photograph has been fabricated.
  - Add real team portraits only with consent and accurate names/roles.

Deploy: open github.com/thapasupreeya3-cpu/bookit-app/upload/main
and drag the contents of this folder into the page (including server.js,
package.json, .gitignore and the public folder). Commit, then: sudo bookit-update

Local check: node server.js, then open http://localhost:3000

Safe to delete from the repo whenever you like (nothing uses them):
  index.html (the one at repo root), DESIGN.md, README-V63.md,
  get-assets.mjs, CARE_BACKGROUND_UPDATE.txt,
  public/assets/world/, public/assets/care-backgrounds/,
  public/assets/care-motion/, public/images/care-poster.webp,
  public/vendor/oo-scrub.js
