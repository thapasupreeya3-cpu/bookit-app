Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Medication Plan and Administration Form

Participant details

- Name, date of birth and contact number, with a photograph of the person
- Representative / emergency contact name(s) and number
- Doctor / prescriber contact number
- Confirmed allergies and reactions, with notes
- Drug sensitivities

The plan lists the medications and the date each was added.

Escalation

Any issues or discrepancies, please contact Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd on 0488 114 368 and the Doctor specified above. If an emergency, use the following process:

- Use the nearest phone to the site of the emergency.
- Call 000 and ask for ambulance.
- State the address.
- Provide the call back number — 0488 114 368.
- Advise the number of patients and symptoms (for example chest pain).
- Advise the state of consciousness.
- If known, advise the patient's name and date of birth.
- Be prepared to meet the ambulance.

Administration record

For each medication, the form records:

- Medication name and its side effects or adverse effects
- Prescribed date, commence date, cease date and route
- Dose, frequency and the doctor or prescriber's signature
- A month grid of days 1 to 31, by time, in which each administration is signed off
