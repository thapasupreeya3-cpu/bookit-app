Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Mealtime Management Plan

The Mealtime Management Plan has been provided to assist the allied health professional (AHP) and support workers to record the participant's mealtime management requirements and preferences. Where required, the GP or AHP should add or subtract sections to suit the person's support needs. A photograph of the person is inserted with the plan.

The person and the plan

- Name, date of birth, address and phone number
- Worker's name
- Whether this is a new plan or a review, with the date
- Who prepared the Mealtime Management Plan: up to three people, each with name, profession and contact details
- A summary of important issues

Allergies

- Precautions: any food allergies are listed here in capital letters
- Response strategies
- PRN medication: all PRN medication for allergy must be administered as per GP or specialist recommendations

Medications

Refer to the person's medication chart or Webster-pak signing sheet for medication preparation and timing. The plan records any special support required to receive medication.

Eating and drinking

For each of eating and drinking, the plan records:

- Consistency
- Alertness: comments regarding level of alertness at mealtimes
- Amounts: refer to dietetic recommendations
- Special diet, for example weight reduction, weight maintenance, phenylketonuria, or lactose free
- Utensils and equipment, with photos if appropriate
- Positioning during meals and after meals, with photos if appropriate
- Assistance required, with photos if appropriate
- Likes and dislikes
- Religious and cultural preferences
- Oral care

Communication

How the person communicates: feeling full, would like more to eat or drink, needing help, likes something, dislikes something, or other.

Behaviour

- How the person usually behaves before meals, during meals, after meals, or otherwise
- Supervision required during mealtimes: whilst eating and drinking, to prevent grabbing food and fluids, and to prevent sharing food with others

Preferences

- Time taken to eat meals: breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks, drinks, or other
- Environment and atmosphere: noise level, table setting, position at the table, companions, lighting, furniture layout, or other
- Participation: menu planning and meal preparation
- Eating out: best atmosphere, favourite meal, favourite drink, favourite venues, and support items to take

Photograph

A photo or photos of the person document how it looks to support the person to eat their meals in the best way possible: the safe and appropriate position for eating and drinking, and the equipment required, including use of clothing protectors, utensils and aids. The date the photo was taken and who took it are recorded.

Review of plan

- A set review, with the date, the professions (AHP) and signature; or
- An as-needed review: the plan will be reviewed following a problem being identified, re-completion of the Nutrition and Swallowing Risk Checklist, and advice from the person's GP.

Note: if the person appears to have difficulty with any support prescribed, immediately contact the health professional responsible for the development of this plan for advice.

Plan endorsement

The plan is endorsed and dated by the person (if they are able), by their family, guardian or person responsible, by each professional involved (profession, name and signature), and by the worker (position, name and signature).

Sharing the plan

A copy of the Mealtime Management Plan must be provided to any persons or organisations who provide support to the person during mealtimes — for example a day placement, school or education centre, family, friends, centre based respite, or holiday provider — recording the date provided and who it was provided to. Consent for sharing this information should be obtained from the person or the person responsible.

Support worker endorsement

Each support worker confirms, with name, signature and date:

- I have read and understood the Mealtime Management Plan and am able to implement it.
- I have received practical training in mealtime management as per requirements in this plan.
- I understand my duty of care regarding positioning, support and monitoring of risk for this person.

A new page must be completed any time the person's Mealtime Management Plan changes.
