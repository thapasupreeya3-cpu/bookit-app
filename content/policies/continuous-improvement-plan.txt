Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Continuous Improvement Plan

The plan records one row per improvement activity, mapped to the NDIS Practice Standards, with the following columns:

- NDIS Practice Standard
- Activity
- How the activity will be achieved
- Updates or changes
- Progress
- Person responsible
- Planned completion date
- Actual completion date
- Review date

Worked example

| NDIS Practice Standard | Activity | How achieved | Updates or changes | Progress | Person responsible |
| Emergency and Disaster Management | Raise staff and volunteer awareness of evacuation plans | Discuss at staff meeting; hold a drill | Participant Emergency Plan updated | Complete | Director |
