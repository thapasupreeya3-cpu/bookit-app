Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Delegation of Authority

The delegation

In compliance with the NDIS Practice Standards, which necessitate a documented system of delegated authority, and following unanimous agreement of all Directors of Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd, this document serves as a formal statement of delegation of authority for the operations of the company.

We hereby delegate the daily operational responsibilities of the company to Supriya Thapa, who holds the position of Director. This delegation includes all roles and responsibilities explicitly outlined in the Director Position Description and is generally limitless except where constrained by non-delegable responsibilities of the board. Supriya Thapa is expected to report directly to the Board.

In carrying out their duties, Supriya Thapa is granted the authority to:

- Delegate some or all of their responsibilities to other suitably qualified individuals as needed, aligning with the roles described in the Director Position Description.
- Establish and assign essential roles required for the successful operation of the company and the delivery of high-quality services to our participants, including but not limited to roles such as Support Workers, in line with the scope, size, and operational nature of the company.
- Optionally, record these delegated roles and their respective responsibilities in the Delegations Register.

In the absence of the Director

In the event of Supriya Thapa's absence, we delegate the day-to-day operations of the company to other key management personnel as a collective entity, or, in the absence or incapacity of such key management personnel, a suitable individual as reasonably identified by the board.

This temporary delegation of authority is activated until Supriya Thapa resumes their role, or until the board decides otherwise. This interim delegation adheres to the specifics, scope, and limitations as described in the Director Position Description, excluding matters which are non-delegable by the board. In deciding upon a temporary Director, the board will take into account the requirements of the Governance and Operational Management Policy and the Director Position Description.

This delegation of authority is to be read and interpreted in conjunction with the company's constitution, the Governance and Operational Management Policy, the Director Position Description, and any other relevant policies. Any queries regarding this document should be directed to the Director or the Board of Directors.

Delegation of Authority Register

The Board affirms the delegations specified in the register below, granting the Director the authority to reasonably adjust these delegations as necessary. This document is valid irrespective of the number of counterparts in which it is signed, with all such counterparts collectively forming a singular document.

| Responsibility | Delegated to |
| Management of day-to-day operations | Director |
| Decisions within approved budget | Director |
| Oversight of personnel and recruitment | Director |
| Negotiation of contracts and partnerships | Director |
| Preparation of financial reports | Key Personnel |
| Development and management of budgets | Key Personnel |
| Oversight of financial risk management | Key Personnel |
| Compliance with financial regulations and standards | Key Personnel |
| Coordination with NDIS participants | Key Personnel |
| Ensuring services meet NDIS participants' needs | Director |
| Preparation of NDIS plans and reports | Key Personnel |
| Compliance with NDIS regulations | Key Personnel |
| Formulation and delegation of essential roles | Director |
| Recording delegated roles and responsibilities | Director |
| Overseeing delivery of high-quality services | Director, Key Personnel |
| Reporting to the board | Director |
| Temporary takeover of Director responsibilities in the absence of the Director | Key Personnel |

While certain responsibilities are primarily assigned to specific roles, effective operation of the company often necessitates collaboration and coordination among the Director and Key Personnel. These delegations should be seen as an indication of lead responsibility rather than a rigid division of duties.

The register is subject to change based on the needs of the organisation, the direction from the Board of Directors, and the evolving environment of the National Disability Insurance Scheme. Any changes will be communicated promptly and updated in this register. Please direct any questions or clarifications to the Director or the Board of Directors.
