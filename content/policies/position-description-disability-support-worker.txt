Position Description

A disability support worker plays a pivotal role in delivering compassionate care, assistance, and support to individuals living with disabilities. Their objective is to empower individuals with disabilities, fostering increased independence, enhancing their overall quality of life, and promoting active engagement within their communities.

Responsibilities and Duties

- Assist participants with various household tasks, such as meal preparation, cleaning, and laundry, to ensure a safe and comfortable living environment.
- Provide hands-on personal care based on individual participant needs, including assistance with bathing, dressing, grooming, and medication management.
- Foster meaningful connections with participants through active listening, companionship, and empathy, promoting mental and emotional wellbeing.
- Conduct regular assessments of participants' needs and progress, maintaining accurate records and promptly reporting any changes or concerns to the appropriate supervisor.
- Encourage and support participants in developing and maintaining their independence and self-confidence by offering guidance and assistance as needed.
- Ensure the safety and wellbeing of participants at all times, following established safety protocols and infection control measures.
- Maintain participant health records and documentation according to Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd's policies, including shift notes on the platform after every shift.

Knowledge and Skills

- A compassionate and patient demeanour.
- Strong interpersonal and communication skills.
- Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
- Physically able to carry out the duties required by the role.
- The ability to build and maintain rapport with participants.

Qualifications and Experience

- A Certificate III or IV in Individual Support, or a Diploma of Community Services, is advantageous.
- Six months' experience as a support worker or coordinator.
- A current driver's licence, where the role includes transport.
- NDIS Worker Screening Check, and a Working with Children Check where applicable.
- Completion of the NDIS Worker Orientation Program and the Supporting People to Stay Infection Free module.
- Current CPR (HLTAID009) and first aid (HLTAID011) certificates.

Acknowledgement

Each worker acknowledges their position description at induction; the acknowledgement is kept in the worker's file.
