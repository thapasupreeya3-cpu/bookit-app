Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Transport and Vehicle Safety Procedure

Introduction

Purpose

Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd is registered to provide Assist-Travel/Transport (registration group 0108). The purpose of this Procedure is to keep participants and Workers safe whenever a Worker transports a participant, and to define the vehicle, driver and conduct requirements that apply.

Scope

This Procedure applies to any Worker who drives a participant anywhere as part of a support, whether in the Worker's vehicle, the participant's vehicle, or a company-arranged vehicle.

Procedures

Driver requirements, before any transport shift

- The Worker holds a current, unrestricted Australian driver licence appropriate to the vehicle. Licences are recorded in The Care Web's credential system, which tracks expiry dates and warns the Worker and management at 30 and 7 days before expiry.
- The Worker declares they are fit to drive: not affected by alcohol, drugs, fatigue, or medication that impairs driving.
- Any licence suspension, disqualification, or relevant traffic charge must be reported to Key Management Personnel immediately; transport duties stop until resolved.

Vehicle requirements

- The vehicle used is registered, comprehensively insured, and roadworthy. Where a Worker uses their own vehicle for participant transport, they provide evidence of registration and comprehensive insurance annually, held in their worker file.
- Seatbelts are worn by everyone, every trip. Child restraints compliant with law are used for any participant who requires them.
- Wheelchairs and mobility aids are stowed or secured using appropriate restraint points; a wheelchair is never occupied in transit unless the vehicle is fitted for it.
- No smoking or vaping in any vehicle used to transport participants.

Conduct during transport

- No mobile phone use while driving, including hands-free where it distracts from participant needs; navigation is set before departure.
- All road rules are followed. Participant preferences (music, temperature, route, breaks) are respected. Transport time is support time.
- Door-to-door means door-to-door: the Worker ensures the participant has safely entered their destination (or is met by a responsible person, where that is the plan) before leaving. A participant is never set down at the kerb and left.
- Kilometres and any transport charges follow the Service Agreement and the NDIS pricing arrangements (Activity Based Transport is itemised separately from support time); charges are transparent to the participant before the trip.

Breakdown, accident or incident on the road

- Safety first: move to safety, hazard lights, 000 for any injury or danger.
- The Worker contacts the office as soon as safe, and alternative transport for the participant is arranged before anything else is dealt with.
- Any accident, near miss or injury during transport is recorded in the incident system within 24 hours; injuries and other serious events follow the Incident Management Policy timelines automatically.

Records

Driver licences (with expiry tracking) live in The Care Web; vehicle insurance evidence lives in worker files; transport-related incidents live in the incident register. These together evidence this Procedure operating.
