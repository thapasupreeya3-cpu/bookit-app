Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Restrictive Practices Register

Purpose

This register records every use of a regulated restrictive practice in relation to a participant supported by Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd, in accordance with the NDIS (Restrictive Practices and Behaviour Support) Rules 2018 and the Behaviour Support and Restrictive Practices Policy. A regulated restrictive practice is seclusion, chemical restraint, mechanical restraint, physical restraint, or environmental restraint.

Nil is the expected state

Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd does not use restrictive practices as a matter of course. A register with no entries is still a register: it must exist, be dated, and be reviewed so the office can show that the nil state was checked rather than assumed. The review below records each check.

Any use of a restrictive practice

- Any use of a regulated restrictive practice that is not authorised under the participant's behaviour support plan and the relevant state or territory authorisation is an unauthorised restrictive practice and a reportable incident, notified to the NDIS Commission within five business days (see the NDIS Reportable Incident Form 5-Day Notification and the Incident Management Policy).
- Every use, authorised or not, is entered below and reported monthly to the NDIS Commission where the Rules require it.
- Where a practice is used, a behaviour support practitioner is engaged and a behaviour support plan is developed or amended.
