Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Participant Exit and Transition Form

Participant details

- First name, last name and contact details

Representative details

- First name, last name, relationship to the client and contact details

Transition details

- Type of transition: exit service, temporary, or other (specified)
- For an exit from service: the date support ends and the reason for exiting
- For a temporary transition: where the participant is transitioning to, the date support ends, the date support resumes, and the reasons for the transition

Information sharing

- The information sharing arrangement
- Whether consent to share information has been provided: Yes / No

Transition risks

The form records the risks associated with the transition, the risk management strategies, comments, and the person responsible for each.

Consent

The Participant consents to Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd:

- Sharing and discussing your Plan with any new providers of your supports and services that you have identified to us, ensuring a well-coordinated and documented transition of supports.
- Engaging in discussions with the new providers you have chosen, including the discussion of any risks associated with transitioning your care, to ensure a smooth and planned transfer of services.
- Providing copies of all existing records related to the supports and services we have provided, except for those records that Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd is not obligated to release under applicable law. This will be done upon your request and is aimed at facilitating an informed transition while respecting privacy and legal requirements.

The form is signed and dated by the participant and by their representative, each with their printed name.
