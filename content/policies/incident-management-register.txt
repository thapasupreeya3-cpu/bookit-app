Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Incident Management Register

This Incident Management Register is intended to capture important incident-related information and should be treated as a confidential document. Please ensure that all incidents are reported, investigated and managed in accordance with the Incident Management Policy.

Each entry records the incident date, time and location (or, if unknown, when and where it was first identified); the incident type (for example abuse or neglect, injury or fall, medication error, behavioural incident); the person involved, the staff involved and any witnesses; whether an incident form was completed and whether the incident was reported to the NDIS Commission or other external bodies (with the reference number and the date and time reported); a detailed description including the impact or harm caused, updated as new information is discovered; the investigation and assessment undertaken under the Incident Management Policy; the actions taken (immediate, preventive, corrective and follow-up); the outcome of the investigation; the affected person's response and any further action; the date resolved; and the Incident Manager's sign-off.

Incidents logged through The Care Web's own incident reporting are also kept in the live incident register on the office board; this page is the register document for incidents recorded by hand.
