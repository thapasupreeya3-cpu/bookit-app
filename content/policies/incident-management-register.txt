Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Incident Management Register

This Incident Management Register is intended to capture important incident-related information and should be treated as a confidential document. Please ensure that all incidents are reported, investigated and managed in accordance with the Incident Management Policy. The register records one row per incident with the following columns:

- Reference number.
- Incident date, time and location — or, if unknown, the time, date and location the incident was first identified.
- Incident type — for example abuse or neglect, injury or fall, medication error, behavioural incident, or other.
- Details of the person involved: name and contact details.
- Staff involved: name and position or role.
- Witnesses: name and contact details.
- Incident form completed? Y/N.
- Incident reported to the NDIS and relevant external bodies? Y/N — if so, the NDIS incident reference number and the date and time the incident was reported.
- Description of the incident: a detailed description, including relevant facts and the impact or harm caused to a person with a disability, updated as new information is discovered.
- Investigation and assessment: details of the assessment or investigation undertaken in accordance with the Incident Management Policy.
- Actions taken in response, including immediate actions, preventive measures, corrective actions and follow-up actions.
- Outcome of the investigation: details or a reference to the investigative report.
- The affected person's response and any further action required.
- Date the incident was resolved.
- Actions taken to prevent further incidents.
- Signature of the Incident Manager: full name, signature and date.
