Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Medication Consent Form

This Medication Consent Form provides detailed information about the medications you require support with, the type of support, and consent for Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd staff to assist.

Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd ensures all participants have current, accurate and reliable records of medication assistance. This is to support the safe management of medication in the community and at home.

The form records the participant's name, date of birth and address, then one row per medication:

- Date the medication was listed
- Type of medication and its name or details
- Dosage
- The support consented to, ticked as applicable: prompt or remind, assist (open packet), or administer or apply

Consent

I, the Participant or their Authorised Representative, hereby give permission and my full consent for Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd staff to assist with medication management to the Participant as detailed above.

In the event additional medications are required to be administered or applied, a new Medication Consent Form will be completed.

I acknowledge that should I refuse to take the medications listed above or choose to administer my own medications, I do so at my own risk and that staff may notify my medical practitioner.

The Consenting Party agrees to allow the Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd staff who are appropriately trained and qualified to carry out the above medication support or assistance, as per the pharmacist's or medical practitioner's instructions and the medication packaging.

The form is signed and dated by the participant or consenting party and by the manager or supervisor, each with their printed name.
