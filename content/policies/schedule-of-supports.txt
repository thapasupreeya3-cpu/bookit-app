Section 1. Supports and pricing

Prices are the NDIS Pricing Arrangements price limits current at the date of service (2026-27 shown as a guide; limits update each 1 July and this Schedule is re-issued). Public holidays are billed at the public-holiday limit. The example row is a guide only — delete it when completing.

The Schedule totals the amount per week and the estimated amount per plan year (weekly multiplied by 52).

Section 2. Weekly roster of care — summary

The live roster (workers, exact times, changes over time) is maintained in The Care Web platform and forms part of this Schedule; every generated shift is recorded against this participant. The summary grid records, for each day Monday through Sunday: day hours, evening hours, and whether there is a sleepover.

Section 3. What is included and what is not

Included: the supports itemised above, delivered per the roster of care, with workers screened, trained and insured by us.

Not included (and never billed to your NDIS plan by us): rent or board, groceries, utilities, personal spending, activity entry fees.

Changes: you can request a change to this Schedule at any time; changes are agreed with you per our Supported Decision-Making Procedure and a re-issued Schedule replaces this one. Where your funding or the NDIS price limits change, we re-issue this Schedule.

Agreement to this Schedule

The Schedule is signed and dated by the participant (or their representative) and by a named representative of Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd.
