Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Medicine Register

The register records the participant's name, date of birth and NDIS number, then one row per movement of medication with these columns:

- Date and time
- Address
- Name of medication
- Name of prescriber
- Dosage (mg/tab)
- Quantity in, quantity out, and balance
- Medication returned / storage locked?
- Signature of worker
- Notes

Storage requirement

Medications must be stored in a secure, lockable location, in original packaging or dispensed medications repackaged in a Dose Administration Aid (DAA) and in accordance with pharmaceutical storage requirements.
