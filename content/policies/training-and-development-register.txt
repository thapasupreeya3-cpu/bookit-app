Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Training and Development Register

Use this register to record formal and informal training received by a worker. It is kept in the administrative file for each worker. Each entry names the worker, so one register serves the whole team; filter or sort by worker name when reviewing.

Formal qualifications and skills

Record formal training from an external provider that has resulted in recognised qualifications, skills or licences — for example Cert III, Cert IV, a Diploma, or CPR training. Documentation sighted might be a certificate from the training provider; comments might note why the qualification is needed for the role.

Register entries: formal qualifications

| Worker | Qualification or skill | Date completed | Training provider | Documentation sighted | Expiry date | Comments |

Task-specific training

Record on-job or informal training where the worker was assessed as competent to perform tasks required for their job — for example manual handling procedures, medication administration, or infection control. How assessed is online, in house, or an external training organisation; the outcome is certification received, further training required, or work experience required.

Register entries: task-specific training

| Worker | Task | How assessed | Trained by | Outcome | Date completed | Feedback | Worker and supervisor sign-off | Review date |

Participant-specific training

Record worker training on participant-specific needs. This may include Support Plans, Emergency Plans, Medication Plans, Mealtime Management Plans, medical emergency protocols, and specific procedures such as in-home evacuation or seizure response. The date completed should be in line with plan reviews or circumstantial changes.

Register entries: participant-specific training

| Worker | Participant | Training on | Trained by | Outcome | Date completed | Feedback | Worker and supervisor sign-off | Review date |
