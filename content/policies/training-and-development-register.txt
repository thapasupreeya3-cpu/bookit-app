Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Training and Development Register

Use this form to record formal and informal training received by a worker. This should be included in the administrative files for the worker. The register records the worker's name and job title, and includes formal qualifications and skills, task-specific training, and participant-specific training.

Formal qualifications and skills

Record formal training from an external provider that has resulted in recognised qualifications, skills or licences — for example Cert III, Cert IV, or a Diploma. The columns are:

- Qualification / skill (for example CPR training)
- Date completed
- Training provider
- Documentation sighted (for example a certificate from the training provider)
- Expiry date (if applicable)
- Comments (for example, needed for the support worker role)

Task-specific training

Record on-job or informal training where the worker was assessed as competent to perform tasks required for their job — for example manual handling procedures, medication administration, infection control. The columns are:

- Task
- How assessed (online, in house, or an external training organisation)
- Trained by
- Outcome (certification received, further training required, or work experience required)
- Date completed
- Feedback
- Worker signature and supervisor signature
- Review date (if applicable)

Participant-specific training

Record worker training on participant-specific needs. This may include but is not limited to Support Plans, Emergency Plans, Medication Plans, Mealtime Management Plans, medical emergency protocols and plans, and specific needs or monitoring arrangements. The columns are:

- Participant
- Training on (for example a Support Plan, Emergency Plan or Epilepsy Plan)
- Trained by
- Outcome (for example, trained on participant-specific needs, in-home evacuation procedures, and response actions to seizures and escalation procedures)
- Date completed (in line with plan reviews or circumstantial changes)
- Feedback
- Worker signature and supervisor signature
- Review date (if applicable)
