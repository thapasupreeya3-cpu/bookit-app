Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Participant Support Plan

Participant information

- Participant name, date of birth, phone, email and address
- Living arrangement (for example alone, with family, in an unmodified or modified home or unit, supported accommodation)
- Today's date, and the scheduled review date (6 to 12 months unless otherwise agreed with the participant)
- Whether this is a first plan or a plan review, and the worker's name
- Is the participant Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander, and do they need access to ATSI services?
- Disability or diagnosis, and health conditions (physical, mental health and so on)
- GP name and contact details (if applicable)
- Is there anyone the participant would like involved in the support plan?
- Does the participant have a representative such as a nominee or guardian, with their name and contact details (if applicable)
- Preferred communication style or contact method, ticking all that apply: face to face, phone, email only, follow up with phone call, Zoom, lots of reminders, do not communicate with me, Easy English, or a social media service

Emergency arrangements

- Has a Participant Emergency Plan been completed in collaboration with the participant? If not, why (for example, not relevant due to online supports)
- Are there any specific support needs to consider if there was an emergency or disaster?

Communication

- Type: verbal, non-verbal, communication aids required, or other
- Are you of a culturally or linguistically diverse background?
- Languages spoken, and whether an interpreter is required
- Any culture, diversity, values and beliefs we should be aware of, with details
- How the participant expresses emerging health concerns, and the systems for escalation in urgent health situations

NDIS funding information

- NDIS plan number, and funding type: self managed, plan managed or NDIA managed
- Plan start and end dates
- Whether appropriate funding is available, and which funds: core supports, capacity building supports, or capital supports
- Plan manager's details (if applicable), and whether a copy of the plan has been provided
- Whether any supports are being provided by another provider, with their details
- Whether the participant is happy for us to collaborate with and share information with other providers to assist in service provision, and which providers

How can we assist you

- Which services are you interested in?
- What are your preventative health needs? Do you need our support to access dentist appointments, vaccinations or other allied health services?

Functional requirements

For each, the plan records whether assistance is needed and the details of the aid or assistance required: housework, transport, shopping, meal preparation, eating, taking oral medication, handling money, telephone, mobility, transfers (bed and chair), bathing and showering, oral care, dressing, grooming (makeup, hair, nails, shaving), and toileting.

Health requirements

For each, whether assistance is needed and the details: medical checkups, dental appointments, allied health appointments, vaccination appointments, continence, skin integrity, swallowing, muscular pain, nerve pain, falls, muscular issues other than pain, and other health concerns.

Behavioural requirements

For each, whether assistance is needed and the details: communication, memory, concentration, planning, decision making, and mood.

Social requirements

For each activity, whether assistance is needed and the activity details (type, time spent, and the assistance required): family, hobbies and interests, religion and spirituality, outings, computer, employment, sports, music, movies and TV, food and alcohol, sex and intimacy, and other.

Mealtime requirements

Each answered Yes or No with details if applicable: allergies, intolerances, vegetarian, vegan, other dietary requirements, whether a meal plan has been prepared, nutrition or swallowing issues, seating and positioning requirements while eating or drinking, and any food preparation requirements.

Your goals

For up to four goals, the plan records the goal and how the participant sees us supporting them with it.

Strengths

What are your strengths? Tell us how we can help utilise them while we support you. Strengths can be things like: I'm patient, I'm empathetic, I am great at time management, I am very loving, I am a hard worker.

Worker matching

We value getting the right worker match for your needs, considering factors like personality, language, culture, and skills. We want you to be part of this matching process and can help you find an advocate if you wish. The plan records the characteristics you would like in your support worker, and anything else you would like us to know.

Administration requirements

Each answered Yes or No:

- Has this document been completed with the participant?
- Has a Risk Assessment Form been completed with the participant?
- Would you agree to let us arrange for a qualified and/or experienced worker from Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd to temporarily provide support to you, should there be an emergency or if your regular worker is unavailable?
- If an emergency arises, would you permit us to collaborate with an external agency or contractor for short-term assistance, or to fill the position if needed?
- If an unplanned absence turns into a permanent one, are you comfortable with us finding a new worker to permanently take on the role?
- Would you like us to share details about your support plan with your family, caregivers, other service providers, and pertinent government agencies?
- Is this support plan stored in a location where the participant can easily access it?
- Is the support plan kept in a place that's easily accessible to Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd?
- Has an individualised plan been developed for this participant in case of emergencies?

Consent

The plan is signed and dated by Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd's representative, the participant's worker, the participant, the participant's representative (if applicable), and an interpreter (if applicable), to indicate consent to the prepared Participant Support Plan.
