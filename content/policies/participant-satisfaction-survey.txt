Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Participant Satisfaction Survey

The purpose of the Participant Satisfaction Survey is to determine our performance from a participant's perspective, and to ensure that Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd is providing services in a manner that supports individual goals and needs. We encourage and appreciate your feedback and comments, and reassure you of your privacy when addressing your concerns. This survey is confidential.

The survey records the participant's name, an optional contact number, the service or services received, a signature and date, and whether they would like to be contacted regarding their feedback.

Statements

Each statement is answered Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Somewhat disagree, Completely disagree, Not sure, or N/A:

- I am treated with respect and dignity in my dealings with Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd. I feel listened to and heard.
- Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd involves me in care planning and the service delivery is tailored to my needs.
- Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd is responsive to my feedback, comments and requests regarding my support plan.
- My language and cultural needs are taken into consideration.
- Information is communicated to me in a way that I can easily understand.
- When Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd makes decisions, I am given enough information to understand the decision.
- Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd ensures that I am given the opportunity to send feedback and complaints, and that I feel safe to do so.
- My feedback and complaints have been dealt with in a timely manner.
- The staff have a high level of knowledge and skills, and meet my needs.
- The staff encourage me to make my own choices and decisions, and be more independent.
- I feel that my information is kept confidential by staff.

Open questions

- If you have lodged a complaint, has your complaint been resolved to your satisfaction? If not, why not?
- If you have an advocate or support person who assists you to carry out your wishes, does Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd include this person in decisions that affect you?
- What do you like about our services?
- What do you dislike about our services?
- Do you have any suggestions of ways we could improve our services?
- Please rate your overall satisfaction with our supports and services, where 1 is lowest and 10 is highest.
- Do you have any other comments?
