Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Job Candidate Interview Form

Interview information

- Candidate's name and interviewer's name
- Date of interview
- Position

Skills and qualifications

- Essential skills
- Qualifications and registrations (if applicable)
- Desirable skills
- Does the candidate meet the necessary skills and qualifications? Yes / No

Questionnaire

- Can you provide a brief overview of your relevant experience and qualifications for the position or role?
- What specific skills or competencies do you possess that make you a strong fit for this position or role?
- How do you approach problem-solving or handling challenging situations in the workplace?
- Can you describe a time when you demonstrated strong teamwork or collaboration skills?
- What are your long-term career goals and how does this position or role align with those goals?

Overall assessment

Each statement is rated Poor, Fair, Good or Excellent:

- The candidate presented relevant qualifications for the applied position.
- The candidate exhibited strong problem-solving skills.
- The candidate displayed effective verbal and written communication skills.
- The candidate's attitude and professionalism align well with the organisation.

Recommendations and outcome

The interviewer records recommendations and one outcome:

- The candidate meets the desirable skills and can fulfil the requirements for the applied position.
- The candidate does not meet the requirements for the applied position.

The form is signed and dated by the interviewer with their printed name.
