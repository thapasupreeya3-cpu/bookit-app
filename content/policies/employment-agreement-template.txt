Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Employment Agreement Template

This is the Letter of Appointment DMHC uses to employ a support worker. Workers engaged through BookIt are employees under the Social, Community, Home Care and Disability Services Industry Award 2010 (SCHADS) and the National Employment Standards. The office completes the position details for the person, attaches the Position Description, the Fair Work Information Statement (and the Casual Employment Information Statement where the employment is casual), a Tax File Number declaration and a superannuation choice form, and both parties sign section C.

Letter of Appointment

Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd (ABN 19 658 578 575, NDIS provider 4-LO5XNY0) is pleased to offer you employment on the terms set out in this letter. Please read it, complete the attached forms, and sign the acceptance at section C.

Part A. Position details

- Employee: full name, date of birth, address, phone and email
- Position: Disability Support Worker (or the position title)
- Reports to: Supriya Thapa, Director
- Employment type: casual, part-time or full-time
- Start date
- Award and classification: Social, Community, Home Care and Disability Services Industry Award 2010 (SCHADS) — classification and pay point, for example Home care employee, Level 3, pay point 1
- Hours: for a casual employee, engaged shift by shift under DMHC's roster with no guaranteed hours; for a part-time employee, the agreed ordinary hours and days
- Pay and superannuation: paid at the applicable SCHADS Award rate for the classification, including the 25% casual loading where employed casually, with superannuation paid to the employee's chosen fund at the statutory rate. Pay is fortnightly by transfer to the nominated account.
- Location: the participant's home and community, and such other locations as DMHC reasonably directs
- Probation: not applicable for casual employment; otherwise the probation period
- NDIS Worker Screening Check: clearance number and expiry date

Part B. Terms of employment

1. Employment. You are employed by DMHC under the SCHADS Award and the National Employment Standards. If casual, each engagement ends at the end of the rostered shift, and casual conversion rights apply as provided by the Fair Work Act.

2. Duties. Your duties are set out in the Position Description – Disability Support Worker, which forms part of this letter, and you must follow each participant's support plan, risk assessment and DMHC's reasonable directions.

3. Worker screening. You must hold a current NDIS Worker Screening Check clearance for the whole period of employment, allow DMHC to verify it directly in the NDIS Worker Screening Database, and tell DMHC immediately of any interim bar, suspension, revocation, exclusion, banning order or allegation of misconduct. You must not perform the role without a current clearance.

4. Conduct and compliance. You must comply with the NDIS Code of Conduct, the NDIS Practice Standards, applicable laws and DMHC's policies and procedures, as acknowledged in the Worker Declarations, and maintain the training and competencies required for the role.

5. Incidents, complaints and reporting. You must notify DMHC immediately and within 4 hours of any reportable or potentially reportable incident, within 24 hours of any other incident, and within one business day of any complaint or feedback, and cooperate with DMHC's incident, complaint and investigation processes, keeping accurate service records, timesheets and file notes.

6. Privacy. You must use participant information only to deliver the support, keep it secure, not disclose it without authorisation, report any suspected breach within 24 hours, and return participant information when employment ends.

7. Ending employment. Notice of termination is as provided by the SCHADS Award and the National Employment Standards. DMHC may suspend or end employment in accordance with the law where there is a concern about participant safety, worker screening or serious misconduct.

8. Entire terms. This letter, the Position Description and DMHC's policies record the terms of your employment. Variations must be in writing. New South Wales law applies.

Part C. Acceptance

I accept employment on the terms set out in this letter, and I confirm I have received the Fair Work Information Statement (and, if casual, the Casual Employment Information Statement), and that I have completed the attached Tax File Number declaration and superannuation choice form.

Signed and dated by the employee, and for DMHC by Supriya Thapa, Director.

Contractors under existing arrangements

Where a direct-delivery worker under an existing arrangement is engaged as a sole-trader contractor rather than an employee, the office uses the Combined Contractor Approval and Services Agreement instead of this letter. That agreement carries the same worker-screening, incident, privacy and insurance terms, records the pre-start checks in the NDIS Worker Screening Database, and confirms that DMHC remains the registered provider that holds the participant's service agreement and makes any NDIS claim. Nothing existing is replaced by this template.
