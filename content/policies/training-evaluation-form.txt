Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Training Evaluation Form

Training details

- Name of worker
- Training topic and date completed
- Training type: online, in house, or an external training organisation

Worker evaluation

Each question is answered Yes or No with comments, or in the worker's own words:

- Was the training successful in fulfilling your learning goals?
- Was the training presented in a clear manner?
- How will you put what you have learnt into practice?
- Do you think there are any areas for improvement in relation to how the training was delivered?
- Do you have any suggestions for further content to be included?
- What did you like most and least about the training?
- Do you need further training on this topic?
- Are there any other topics you would like further training on?
- Any additional feedback

The worker signs the evaluation.

Office and manager use

- Have improvement opportunities been identified and documented on the Continuous Improvement Register? Yes / No
- Has the training been recorded on the worker's Training and Development Register? Yes / No
- The date set for observing the employee applying the training they have completed
- The outcome of the observation, with the date undertaken: the employee has effectively implemented the training and demonstrates competency in this area; further training is required; or work experience is required
- Comments and signature
