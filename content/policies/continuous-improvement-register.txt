Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Continuous Improvement Register

To be completed in accordance with the Continuous Improvement Policy. Each improvement activity records a unique item number; the date the opportunity was identified and its source (for example client feedback, a staff suggestion, or audit findings); a description of the current issue or opportunity; the desired outcome — what successful improvement would look like; the responsible party; an action plan summarising the steps to be taken, including resources required and any dependencies or risks; the target date; the current status (planning, in progress, completed, on hold); the outcome and evaluation of effectiveness once implemented, including any measures of success; any follow-up actions (for example training needs or communication to stakeholders); and the date closed.
