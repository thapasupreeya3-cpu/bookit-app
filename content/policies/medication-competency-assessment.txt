Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Medication Competency Assessment

This checklist provides a structured framework for assessing medication administration competency, including knowledge, practical skills, safety, documentation, communication, and overall performance.

This checklist is to be completed after each worker has completed medication training, and prior to supporting a participant with medication administration.

Assessment details

- Name of worker
- Does the worker possess current medication training? Yes / No
- Date assessment completed
- Assessor and assessor position

Knowledge assessment

Each item is answered Yes or No, with comments:

- Demonstrates understanding of medication names, classifications, indications, and contraindications.
- Able to accurately calculate medication dosages and measurements.
- Understands potential side effects and adverse reactions associated with administered medications.
- Aware of proper storage requirements for medications.
- Knowledgeable about medication administration routes and techniques (for example oral, injectable, topical).

Practical skills assessment

- Able to correctly identify medications based on label, appearance, and documentation.
- Demonstrates proper hand hygiene before medication administration.
- Follows correct patient identification procedures.
- Administers medications accurately according to prescribed dosage and route.
- Demonstrates appropriate technique for medication administration (oral, application).

Safety and documentation

- Adheres to safety protocols throughout the medication administration process.
- Demonstrates proper disposal of used supplies.
- Accurately documents medication administration, including dosage, route, time, and any relevant patient responses or observations.
- Recognises and responds appropriately to medication errors or adverse reactions.
- Demonstrates knowledge of reporting procedures for medication errors and adverse events.
- Seeks clarification or assistance when uncertain about medication orders or procedures.

Assessment outcome

The assessor records one of the following, with comments and a signature:

- The employee has effectively implemented medication administration and demonstrates competency in this area.
- Further training is required.
