Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Emergency Test Register

This register should be implemented in accordance with the Emergency and Disaster Management Policy. Each test of an emergency plan is numbered and records the type of test, which emergency plan it exercised, when it ran, the notes and recommendations arising, the results, and the tester's sign-off.
