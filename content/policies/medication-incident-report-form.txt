Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Medication Incident Report Form

Reporting details

- Name and position of the person reporting
- Date the incident was identified
- Date and time the incident occurred
- Name of the person responsible for the incident or error (if not the person reporting)
- Date reported to management

Participant details

- Participant's name
- Location of the incident

The incident

- Type of incident or error — for example wrong participant, wrong medication, wrong dose, wrong time, wrong route, incorrect documentation, pharmacy error, or other (specified)
- Details of the incident or error
- Causes or contributing factors — for example missing information, missing labels, storage, training deficit, environmental, or other (specified)
- How the incident was managed — for example notified the Director, notified the participant (and/or their authorised representative), notified the GP, telephoned an ambulance, or other (specified)
- Follow up actions

Sign-off

The form is signed and dated by the person reporting the issue and by the Supervisor or Director, each with their name.
