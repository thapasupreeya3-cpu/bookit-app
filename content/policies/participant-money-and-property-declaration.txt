Consent to use of money and property

I, the named participant, require financial assistance and grant my approval to Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd for the provision of such support. Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd is authorised to use my cash, credit and debit cards, and other assets under the conditions that:

- A comprehensive written record is maintained for all transactions involving the use of my funds.
- All bank documents, credit cards, cash and cheque books are kept secure when in their possession or control.
- Documentation, including receipts, is prepared for all participant-related expenditures.
- Strict adherence to the Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd Privacy and Information Management Policy and Participant Money and Property Policy is maintained, ensuring confidentiality and compliance.

Incident Reporting for Financial Abuse or Exploitation

Any breach or suspected misuse of participants' funds will be treated with the utmost seriousness. Each incident must be reported and managed in accordance with our incident management system, as outlined in our Incident Management Policy. Further details can be found in the policy document.

Participants are encouraged and supported in seeking legal or advocacy services when allegations of financial abuse or exploitation arise.

For inquiries, concerns or to request copies of our policies (the Participant Money and Property Policy, Privacy and Information Management Policy, Feedback and Complaints Policy, or Incident Management Policy), please contact Supriya Thapa at Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd on 0488 114 368.

Can I Withdraw or Amend my Consent for Money Usage?

You have the right to withdraw or amend your consent to Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd using your funds and assets at any time by submitting a written notice to Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd.
