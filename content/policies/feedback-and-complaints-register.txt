Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Feedback and Complaints Register

This Feedback and Complaints Register should be completed in accordance with the Feedback and Complaints Policy. Each entry records when the complaint was received and how (in person, by phone, or by post); the reference number (the internal complaint form or the NDIS reference); whether it was anonymous; the complainant's name and contact details (and their relationship to the participant if a representative); a full description; the response, the reason for the action or decision, and the dates actioned and completed; the status of the process; the participant's satisfaction with the outcome; and the Complaint Manager's sign-off.

Complaints lodged through the BookIt feedback form are also kept in the live complaints register on the office board; this page is the register document for complaints recorded by hand.
