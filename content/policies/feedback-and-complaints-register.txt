Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Feedback and Complaints Register

This Feedback and Complaints Register should be completed in accordance with the Feedback and Complaints Policy. The register records one row per complaint with the following columns:

- Date and time
- Mode of complaint: in person, phone, or by post
- Reference number: reference to the internal complaint form or the NDIS reference number
- Anonymous (Y/N): was the complaint submitted anonymously?
- Name and contact details: name, phone number, email, and the relationship to the complainant if a representative
- Description of complaint: full description of the complaint
- Response action and reason: details of the response and reasons for the action or decision
- Date actioned and date completed
- Status: status of the complaint process
- Complaint satisfaction: details of the participant's response and satisfaction with the outcome
- Complaint Manager signature: full name and signature of the Complaint Manager, and the date signed
