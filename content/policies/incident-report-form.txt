Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Incident Report Form

This form is to be used for reporting incidents, in accordance with the Incident Management Policy.

If you are reporting on a reportable incident, or are not sure whether this may be a reportable incident, please immediately contact the incident manager or other key personnel.

Part A

To be completed by a worker who has become aware of or witnessed the incident.

Incident report details

- Details of the worker the incident was first reported to: name, position and contact details
- Report date
- Has this incident been reported to the Incident Manager? If yes: the Incident Manager, the time and date notified, and how (phone call, email or other)

The affected participant

- Title, surname and given names
- Address, phone, date of birth and email
- Next of kin: surname and given name

The incident, as applicable:

- Acts, omissions, or events occurring in relation to providing supports
- Acts by a person with a disability
- Have or could have caused harm
- Have caused serious harm or risk of harm to another person

Incident details

- Date (or disclosure date) of the event, time and location
- Type of incident, ticking the most relevant: an incident that resulted in harm or risk of harm to a participant, or an incident caused by a participant resulting in serious harm or risk of serious harm to another person
- Reportable incidents: death of a participant; serious injury to a participant; abuse or neglect of a participant; unlawful physical contact or assault of a participant; unlawful sexual contact with or sexual assault of a participant; sexual misconduct committed against or in the presence of a participant; use of an unauthorised restrictive practice in relation to a participant
- Non-NDIS and other incidents: work health and safety incident, infection, hazardous exposure, child safety, or other

Describe the incident

A detailed description of the incident, including who was involved, what happened and how it occurred or was alleged to have occurred, and why it occurred (if known). The form also records whether this is a reportable incident, whether it is an incident regarding child safety, and if so whether the Incident Manager has been notified.

For injuries

- Nature of injury: contusion or crush, burn, dislocation, amputation, laceration or open wound, superficial injury, foreign body, internal injury, concussion, sprain or strain, fracture, or dermatitis
- Location of injury: head or face, eye, internal organs, hand or fingers, shoulder or arms, trunk (other than back), hip or leg, foot or toes, back, or other

Immediate actions and witnesses

- Immediate actions taken to make the situation safe, including any medical treatment received or assistance provided or offered
- Witnesses, with names and phone numbers — for a notifiable incident, attach signed witness statements or letters of complaint

Part B

The investigation, to be completed by the Incident Manager or other key personnel. Always ensure the persons affected by the incident are considered during the investigation.

- Outcome of the investigation
- For reportable incidents: was the incident reported to the NDIS, and on what date?
- For child safety incidents: was the incident reported to Child Safety's local regional intake service or Child Safety Service Centre, and which centre and date?
- Was the incident reported to any other external agencies, and which agency and date?

Please note: NDIS reportable incidents must be reported to the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission within 24 hours, except in the case of an unauthorised restrictive practice where no harm or injury has resulted, which must be reported in 5 days.

Prevention and review

- Actions to be taken to prevent further similar incidents from reoccurring or to minimise their impact — each with the action, the responsibility, and the completion date
- Have all relevant parties been spoken to and consulted, and on what date?
- Was the incident preventable?
- How well was the incident managed, and how well was it resolved?
- Do other parties or external agencies need to be notified of the outcome?
- Has the participant been consulted and feedback sought regarding: how well the incident was managed, whether they received adequate support, expectations for appropriate resolution, updates on the investigation, outcomes and findings, and being informed of actions taken — with the participant's feedback described
- Was the incident discussed at a team meeting, and on what date?

The form closes with the investigator's name and signature and the Incident Manager's signature. The completed form is to be stored and the incident recorded in the Incident Management Register.
