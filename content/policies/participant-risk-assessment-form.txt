Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Participant Risk Assessment Form

Participant details

- Given names, family name, preferred name and date of birth
- Address, phone number, email address and preferred contact method
- Participant requirements and preferences: preferred communication mode and language, cultural requirements, religious or belief-based requirements, interests, and physical requirements
- Known medical conditions or allergies, each with its effect and treatment

Emergency contacts and carer or guardian details

- Emergency contact names, phone and email
- Carer or guardian family name, given names, email address, phone number and postal address
- In-person contact, with the frequency specified for participants who live alone

Persons involved in the risk assessment

- Was the participant involved in the assessment? Yes, declined (with reason), or unable (with reason)
- Staff involved and others involved
- Daily personal activities, for participants living alone: whether there is a sole support worker, and the reason (participant preference or other). If yes, a Monitoring and Supervision Plan is required.

Information sharing and privacy

- Privacy Policy explained, with comments or feedback
- Consent to share information documented
- Other providers, listed, with comments

Risk identification

For daily personal activities support for participants living alone, the assessment ticks all applicable risk factors:

- Personal contact: no regular face-to-face contact with other NDIS providers; limited or irregular face-to-face contact with relatives, friends or other people.
- Physical mobility: relies on other people to be physically mobile or to facilitate their physical mobility; uses equipment to enable them to be physically mobile or to facilitate their physical mobility.
- Communication: without the assistance of another person the participant has limited or no ability to communicate; the participant uses equipment to enable or facilitate communication with others, including to enable or facilitate the use of a phone or other device.

If supports will be delivered by a sole support worker and any of the above risks apply, a Monitoring and Supervision Plan must be created.

The assessment also records to what degree the participant relies on our services, and how the participant's health and safety would be impacted if their service was disrupted.

Risk categories

All applicable risks are ticked in each category:

- Medical conditions and interventions: fractures and cuts, bruising and abrasions, seizures, respiratory conditions, allergies, skin conditions, endocrine conditions, diabetes, sleep disorders, constipation, incontinence, dementia, obesity, teeth and gum conditions, night-time checking required, missed appointments, medication, not taking medication, declining to participate in medical examinations or procedures, declining to follow medical advice, infectious disease, or other.
- Personal care: feeding, toileting, showering and bathing, dental hygiene, shaving, grooming, or other.
- Eating and drinking: swallowing difficulty, choking on food, enteral feeds (plus oral intake or nil by mouth), food allergies, specialised diet, texture modified diet, thickened fluids, overnight feeds required, food refusal, dehydration, posture and positioning, alertness, modified utensils or equipment, behaviour related to eating or drinking, pica (eating non-food items), environment, or other.
- Accidental movement: startle reflex, panic behaviour, grabbing, holding or leaning, sudden body movements, falling or tripping, bumping or running, or other.
- Manual handling: transfers, mobility, vehicle access, moving in bed, personal care tasks, or other.
- Environmental risks: electrocution, fire lighting and flammables, smoking, sharps and knives, poisons, water hazard and bathing, sun exposure, absconding and wandering, traffic (roads and rail), travel (private or public transport), or other.
- Mental health and wellbeing: suicide risk, self-harm or self-injury, mental health diagnosis, self-neglect, hoarding, or other.
- Financial risks: low income, limited understanding of money, challenges developing and sticking to a budget, vulnerability to financial exploitation, losing a wallet, purse or bag, debt, gambling, or other.
- Social risks: exploitation, unsafe sex, physical abuse or threats, verbal abuse or threats, harassment or stalking, emotional abuse, sexual abuse or threats, neglect, use of projectiles or weapons, property damage, harm to animals, domestic violence, criminal or illegal behaviour, social isolation, lack of informal supports, strangers, anti-social peers, other housemates, other visitors to the home, family and carers, discrimination, homelessness, leaving care, or other.
- Substance use: drugs, medication misuse, alcohol, smoking, or other.
- Other identified risks.

Monitoring and supervision plan

- Is personal support (assistance with daily personal activities) being provided by a sole worker? Yes / No
- If so, the frequency at which Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd will undertake in-person supervision of the worker — weekly, monthly, bi-monthly, quarterly or six-monthly, dependent on the individual circumstances.
- If so, the frequency that will be used to engage with providers who may be involved in providing other support services to the client in their home or in supporting the client to access community based activities.

Risk management plan

A risk management plan is developed for all identified risks, recording for each: the type of risk, the likely effect level on the participant and on others, the risk treatment, the person responsible, and the review date.

The form is signed and dated by the participant or their representative and by the manager or supervisor, each with their printed name.

Risk assessment review

Each review records the date, the reason for review (scheduled review, feedback, or a change in participant needs or circumstances), the outcome and actions to be taken (a new risk assessment completed, or no changes required with the next scheduled review date), who completed the review, and their signature.
