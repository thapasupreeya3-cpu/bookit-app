Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Legislation Register

Introduction

Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd understands that delivering quality and safe services is rooted in strong compliance measures. We are dedicated to upholding all pertinent regulatory, legislative, and contractual obligations. This legislation register is to be read in conjunction with Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd's policies.

In developing this register, Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd has undertaken extensive research into legislation relevant to the NDIS. This includes not only legislation directly applicable to the services outlined in our current business plan, but also legislation that may not presently apply. This broader approach has been adopted to ensure we understand the full legislative landscape and are well positioned should we wish to expand our service offerings in future. Our intent is to be as thorough as possible and ensure our operations are both compliant and future-proofed.
