Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Training and Development Plan

All training is to be recorded on individual personnel Training and Development Registers.

The plan

| Frequency | Title | Description of training | Method | Duration | Who |
| Ongoing | NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission Worker Orientation | Quality, Safety and You online module | Online | 2 hours | All staff |
| Annual | Incident reporting | Incident reporting, minor and major | Internal or online | 1 hour | Support staff |
| Annual | Participants' rights charter | Right to access an independent advocate; participants' complaints procedure; recognising signs of abuse to participants, responding to abuse and reporting; freedom from abuse policy | Internal, by the Director | 2 hours | Support staff |
| Annual | Restrictive intervention and positive behaviour planning | The different types of restrictive intervention (physical, environmental, seclusion, chemical, mechanical), who can authorise restrictive interventions, who can implement them, and what the documentary requirements are in terms of maintaining records | External: the authorised program officer leads formal training to staff | 2 to 3 hours | Support staff |
| Ongoing | Development areas identified in annual performance reviews | As required | Internal or external | As required | All staff |
| Annual | Cultural sensitivity and anti-discriminatory procedures | Training to understand different cultures and linguistically diverse backgrounds; Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander cultures | Centre for Culture, Ethnicity and Health (ceh.org.au) online training | 1 to 3 hours | Support and admin staff |
| Annual | Person-centred planning | Setting goals; strategies for goal achievement; assessment and task analysis; evidence-based outcomes | Internal, management | 2 to 3 hours | Support staff |
| Ongoing | In-house computer training | Basic computer training for staff who are not comfortable using IT equipment: office software, printing, web browsing, smartphones | Internal, management | 1-hour sessions as needed | Admin staff |
| Annual | New participant introduction to services and assessment | Introduction to services for participants; participant induction handbook; participants' rights, choice and control, complaints procedure; participants' service agreement; understanding the NDIS price guide and communicating with participants | Internal, management | 1 to 2 hours | Intake staff |
| Annual | CPR refresher | CPR refresher required for all program staff | External first aid training provider | 1 day | Support staff |
| Annual | Infection prevention and control | Annual training for infection prevention and control procedures, including a refresher | External | 1 hour | All staff |
| Annual | PPE training | Training on the correct use of PPE | Internal, management | 1 hour | Support staff |
| Annual or as needed | Autism training | Diagnosis and prevalence; sensory processing differences; cognitive theories; communication strategies and scenarios | External | To be advised | Support staff |
| Annual | Medication training | Training staff in medication management for participants | To be advised | To be advised | Support staff |
| Annual | Food handling | Basic food handling procedure and food poisoning information | External | 2 hours | Support staff |
| Annual | Mealtime management | Methods and procedures to texture-modify foods; how to respond to incidents such as coughing and choking; proactively managing emerging and chronic health risks related to mealtime difficulties (swallowing difficulties, diabetes, anaphylaxis, food allergies, obesity or being underweight) including how to seek help; how to check that meals are of the correct texture as identified in the plans; participant-specific training with the qualified health practitioners who developed the plan | To be advised | To be advised | Support staff |
| Annual | Fire warden and emergency response | Training for the nominated fire warden staff | External | 1 day | Support staff |
| Annual and at induction | Emergency and disaster management | Training staff in emergency and disaster management and conducting mock drills | External | 2 days | Support staff |
| Quarterly and as new sessions become available | NDIS-focused workshops | NDS-facilitated workshops and NDIA information sessions, focused mainly on NDIS basics | NDIA-run sessions and internal, management | 2 to 3 hours | Admin and management |
| Annual | Information sessions: advocacy and rights | Advocacy; choice and decision-making; the Disability Act; participants' rights and responsibilities; staying safe | Internal | As required | Support staff and management |
| Annual and at induction | Information sessions: zero tolerance | Violence, abuse, neglect, discrimination and exploitation | Internal, management | 1 to 2 hours | All staff |
| Annual and at induction | Information sessions: feedback and complaints | How to receive, report and monitor feedback and complaints | Internal, management | 1 to 2 hours | All staff |
| Annual | Medication competency, support staff | Medication competency assessment | Registered nurse | 1 hour | Support staff |
| Annual | Medication competency, nurses | Medication competency for registered and enrolled nurses | External body | 1 hour | Clinical staff |
| Annual | Information sessions: waste | Management of waste | Internal, management | 1 hour | Support staff |
| Annual, at induction and after any emergency | Information sessions: emergency and disaster management | Emergency and disaster management | Internal, management | As required | All staff |
| Annual | WHS training | Overview of the organisation's work health and safety procedures | Internal | As required | Support staff |
| Annual | Refresher of policies and procedures | Including but not limited to HR policies, freedom from abuse, restrictive intervention, complaints management, incident management and risk management | Internal | As required | Support staff |
| Annual | General professional training | People skills, customer service, collaboration skills and empathy | Internal, management | 1-hour sessions | Support staff |
| Annual | NDIS standards | Working through the NDIS Practice Standards and our self-assessment | Internal, management | Multiple sessions of 2 hours | Support staff |
| Annual | Child safety standards | Working through the child safe standards | Internal, management, and online | Multiple sessions of 1 hour | Support staff |
| Annual and at induction | Participant money and property | Ensuring workers understand this outcome and do not give unsolicited financial advice | Internal, management | As required | Support staff |
