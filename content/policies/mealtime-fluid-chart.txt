Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Mealtime - Fluid Chart

Use this chart to record the participant's intake and output each shift.

The chart records the participant's name, phone number, date of birth, carer or emergency contact details, address and allergies.

The chart

One row per hour, from 0100 through 2400, with a total row at the end. Each row records:

- Intake — oral (description and mL), enteral / PEG feed (feed type and mL), other (description and amount), and the progressive intake total.
- Output — urine via IDC, SPC or intermittent catheter (total mL after emptying the catheter), vomit (approximate amount), bowel (small, medium or large), other, and the progressive output total in mL.

Key abbreviations

- PEG: Percutaneous Endoscopic Gastrostomy
- NGT: Nasogastric Tube
- JEJ: Jejunostomy tube
- IDC: Indwelling Catheter
- SPC: Suprapubic Catheter
- IMC: Intermittent Catheter
- PUIT: Passed Urine in Toilet
