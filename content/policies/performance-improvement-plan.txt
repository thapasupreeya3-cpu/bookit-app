Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Performance Improvement Plan

A performance improvement plan is a document that sets out what the issue is with a Worker's performance and what they need to do to improve it.

Details

- Worker name, position and level
- Manager name and position
- Date of plan and period of plan
- Interim review date and final review date

For each performance improvement objective

- Performance improvement objective: describe the specific area in which the Worker's performance needs to improve — for example, timely and accurate processing of orders.
- Required outcomes: describe what the Worker needs to do to improve their performance to the required standard. The required outcomes should be specific, measurable and realistic; in most cases they should be described as a measurement of quality, quantity or timeliness.
- Strategies: describe how the Worker is going to meet the required outcomes.
- Support: describe what support you're going to provide to the Worker to meet the required outcomes.
- Responsibilities: describe the responsibilities of the Worker, their manager and any other relevant parties.
- Consequences: describe the consequences if the Worker doesn't meet the required outcomes by the final review date.

These sections are repeated for additional objectives. The plan is signed and dated by the manager and the worker, each with their printed name.

Worker self-assessment

The Worker completes this before the interim and final review meetings: how do you think you have performed against each of your performance improvement objectives, and any other comments. Signed and dated with printed name.

Manager assessment

The manager completes this before the interim and final review meetings: how do you think the Worker has performed against each of their performance improvement objectives, and any other comments. Signed and dated by both the manager and the worker, each with their printed name.
