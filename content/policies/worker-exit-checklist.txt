Disability and Mental Health Care Pty Ltd | Worker Exit Checklist

The checklist records the worker's name, who completed it, and the date. Each item is marked Yes, No or N/A.

Before the worker leaves

Have you asked the worker to:

- Complete any reasonable tasks and handover?
- Save important files and emails in a shared drive?

Did you consider:

- Assigning any ongoing tasks to another person?
- Conducting an exit interview to get final feedback from the worker?
- Organising a farewell party?

Last day

Have you collected:

- The worker's keys?
- Company assets (computer, phone, uniform, books and so on)?

Have you set up:

- Auto-forward and an out-of-office auto-reply on their emails?
- Auto-forward and an out-of-office message on their voicemail?

Have you calculated and paid:

- The worker's final pay, including holiday leave owed?

After the worker has left

Have you removed:

- The worker from email distribution lists and contact lists?
- The worker's access to online systems, for example the finance system and the shared file server?

Have you updated the worker's personnel file with:

- Final day of work?
- Last pay amount?
- Holiday pay details?

Have you paid:

- Your worker's final pay, including holiday leave owed, on or before their final payday?
