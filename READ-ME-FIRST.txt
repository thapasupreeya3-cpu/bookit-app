WHY YOU DIDN'T SEE ANY FORMS

Because I hadn't put any there. The last zip built the shelf and handed you
eleven PDFs with instructions to upload them one at a time through the admin
screen. That isn't "the forms are on the site", it's a chore list — and a
chore list is a thing that doesn't get done.

Now the blanks ship in the repository, in forms/, named for the row they
belong to, and the first boot after a deploy files any that are missing. No
uploading. Deploy and they're there.

Seeded once and once only. There's a marker per form, so an administrator who
deliberately removes a blank doesn't find it back the next morning — that's
the failure mode of every auto-import ever written, and I tested for it:
removed one, restarted, it stayed gone. Replacing a blank through the admin
screen simply overwrites the row and the shipped copy stops arguing with it.
