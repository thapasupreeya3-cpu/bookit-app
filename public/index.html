Home / Support services / Employment support
0102 · Assist Access/Maintain Employ

Support to find a job — and keep loving it

Meaningful work changes everything. Our workers back you through every step: exploring what you want to do, landing the role, and building the skills and routines to thrive in it.

Find employment support Browse workers

What this support can include

  • Working out your strengths, interests and job goals
  • Writing résumés, applications and practising interviews
  • Travel training for your commute
  • On-the-job support while you settle in and learn tasks
  • Building workplace routines, timekeeping and stamina
  • Support to communicate with your employer about adjustments
  • Keeping things on track when work gets wobbly
  • Support in ADEs, open employment or self-employment

What a typical shift looks like

Tuesday, 8:30am: your worker meets you at home, runs through the morning checklist, travels with you to work, and stays for the first two hours while you open up the café — prompting where needed, stepping back where not. Over the term, their support fades as your independence grows. That's the goal.

💡 How it's funded

Delivered under registration group 0102 — Assistance to Access and Maintain Employment, usually from your Capacity Building (Finding & Keeping a Job) or Core budget as "supports in employment".

Weekday rate: $73.58/hr — the 2026–27 NDIS price limit
Evenings, weekends & public holidays billed at the applicable NDIS rate

The workers you'll meet

  • Experience supporting people into open employment
  • Patient coaches for new tasks and routines
  • Confident communicating with employers
  • Matched to your industry where possible

0107 · Assist-Personal Activities

Personal care, on your terms

The most personal support deserves the most trust. Choose workers you're comfortable with, brief them your way, and keep the same familiar faces — morning, evening or overnight.

Find personal care Browse workers

What this support can include

  • Showering, bathing and personal hygiene
  • Dressing, grooming and getting ready for the day
  • Support at mealtimes, eating and drinking
  • Day-to-day medication management and reminders
  • Toileting and continence support
  • Transfers and mobility around your home
  • Morning and evening routines
  • Overnight support — active or sleepover

Higher-intensity needs?

We currently support standard-intensity personal care. If you need complex supports (like PEG feeding or complex bowel care under registration group 0104), talk to us — we'll help you plan the right mix and connect you with specialist providers where needed.

💡 How it's funded

Delivered under registration group 0107 — Assistance with Daily Personal Activities, from your Core budget (Assistance with Daily Life).

Evening $81.07 · Sat $103.54 · Sun $133.50 — per the NDIS price guide

Home / Support services / Travel & transport
0108 · Assist-Travel/Transport

Wherever you're going, we'll get you there

Appointments, work, uni, the shops, the game. Ride with a checked and insured worker — or learn to make the trip independently, with support that fades as your confidence grows.

Find transport support Browse workers who drive

What this support can include

  • Transport to and from appointments, therapy and school or work
  • Workers who drive their own insured vehicle
  • Support in your vehicle, taxis or rideshare
  • Public transport travel training — routes, Opal, timetables
  • Accompanied travel to events, family and community activities
  • Errand runs — shopping, pharmacy, post office
  • Wheelchair-accessible planning and door-to-door support
  • Building confidence for independent travel over time

Extra checks for driving

Workers who transport clients hold a current Nationally Coordinated Criminal History Check, a valid licence, registration and comprehensive insurance — verified before their profile shows the "drives" badge.

💡 How it's funded

Delivered under registration group 0108 — Assistance with Travel/Transport arrangements, typically from your Core budget. Worker time is billed at our standard rates; a per-kilometre vehicle allowance applies when your worker drives.

Worker time: $73.58/hr weekday — the NDIS price limit
Vehicle allowance: $1.00/km (activity-based transport)

Good matches for transport

  • Confident, careful drivers with local knowledge
  • Patient trainers for public transport skills
  • Workers who share your schedule — early starts, late finishes

A 2-hour booking minimum applies, in line with SCHADS minimum-engagement rules — the exact price is always shown before you confirm. Sleepovers are billed at the NDIS night-time sleepover rate of $311.79 per night (includes up to 2 hours of active support; extra active hours at the applicable hourly rate). House-cleaning-only bookings are billed at $60.10/hr — the dedicated Household Tasks price limit. Employment support (0102) is billed at its own flat NDIS limit of $83.87/hr, whatever the day or time. Remote and very remote loadings follow the NDIS Pricing Arrangements. Support services are GST-free.

Where your money goes

Every dollar, accounted for

Of your $73.58 weekday hour:

  • $53.25 (72%) — your worker's pay and super, above the SCHADS Award.
  • $10.60 — insurances, workers' comp, screening, training and compliance.
  • $9.73 — the platform, our support team, payroll and claiming.

Compare that with traditional agencies, where workers often see barely 55–60% of what you're charged.

Cancellations, plainly

  • More than 2 clear business days' notice: free, always.
  • Short notice: the shift may be charged, in line with the NDIS Pricing Arrangements — and your worker is still paid.
  • We cancel on you? Never charged, and we'll help find cover fast.

What's always included

  • All insurances for every booked shift
  • Verification, screening and training of workers
  • Automatic invoicing and NDIS claiming
  • Our support team, 7 days
  • 24/7 incident line

Home / Safety & quality

Safety isn't a feature. It's the foundation.

Everything on BookIt is built to the NDIS Practice Standards — from how we screen workers to how we respond at 3am when something isn't right.

Screened before they start

NDIS Worker Screening Check, 100 points of ID, work rights, references, and a live video interview with our onboarding team. Drivers add a police check and insurance; anyone supporting under-18s holds a WWCC.

Employees, fully insured

Workers are our employees on every shift — covered by workers' compensation, public liability and professional indemnity insurance. No gaps, no grey areas, no risk sitting with you.

Trained, and kept trained

Paid orientation covering safeguarding, boundaries, privacy and person-centred practice — plus ongoing modules (manual handling, mealtime safety, positive behaviour basics) tracked on every profile.

24/7 incident support

A real person answers, any hour. Incidents are logged, managed and reported in line with the NDIS Commission's rules — and we stay with you until it's resolved.

Safe ways to speak up

Easy feedback and complaints channels — in the app, by phone, or anonymously. Advocates welcome. Complaints never affect your access to support.

Your privacy, protected

Data encrypted and stored in Australia. You control what's visible on your profile, and we never sell your information. Fully compliant with the Privacy Act and NDIS Practice Standards.

Extra safeguards if you live alone

When one worker regularly supports someone who lives alone, we follow additional safeguards set by the NDIS Commission — and we think they're just good practice. Before support starts we assess any risk factors with you and put a written service agreement in place. While support runs, a supervisor visits your home at an agreed frequency, and someone other than your worker checks in regularly to make sure you're happy with the type, quality and frequency of your support. Your feedback goes straight to our senior team.

Accessibility

Our accessibility commitments

Accessibility isn't an add-on for us — most of our community relies on it. This website is built to WCAG 2.2 AA and comes with tools to make it work your way.

  • Adjust it yourself — text size up to 150%, high contrast, an easier-to-read font, reduced motion and read-aloud, all in the Accessibility options panel at the top of every page.
  • Built-in good practice — full keyboard navigation, visible focus, skip links, screen-reader labels, plain language and strong colour contrast throughout.
  • Other formats on request — large print, Easy Read or translated versions of any of our documents. Just ask, and we'll arrange interpreters (including Auslan) for any conversation.
  • Tell us when we miss — accessibility feedback goes straight to the top of our fix list.

Home / About us

Support should feel like it's on your side

BookIt was started by people who'd lived both sides of the roster — participants tired of strangers at the door, and workers tired of being treated like line items.

Our story

Built from lived experience

We watched brilliant, funny, capable people reduced to "shifts to fill". We watched great workers burn out as contractors with no super, no cover and no say. And we watched families spend their evenings chasing invoices instead of living their lives.

So we built the platform we wished existed: one where participants choose their people, workers are employed properly, and the boring-but-critical stuff — screening, insurance, payroll, claiming — just happens in the background.

We deliberately do six things, not sixty. Employment support, personal care, transport, daily living, household tasks and community participation — the everyday supports that add up to an ordinary, extraordinary life. We'd rather be the best in the country at those than average at everything.

What we believe

  • Choice and control are non-negotiable. You pick your people, full stop.
  • Good support is a relationship, not a transaction.
  • Workers deserve real jobs — fair pay, super, insurance, respect.
  • Transparency builds trust. Our prices, pay and policies are public.
  • Accessibility is table stakes — in our supports and in our software.

Who we serve

NDIS participants of every age and plan type, their families and nominees, support coordinators building reliable teams — and the support workers who make it all real.
